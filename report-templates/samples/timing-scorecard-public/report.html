What this scorecard does not show

  • The oscillator path is each clock's T1 holdover prediction (mean and envelope), not a recorded run under attack; the T1 time to breach is not a validated claim (V5 and V5b missed).
  • Level 1 assumes the clock re-locks to genuine signals once the threat is removed and its alarm is acted on; a modelled run cannot show the re-lock.
  • Downstream clocks receive the grandmaster's error plus their own present servo offset; path asymmetry and packet delay variation are not modelled.
  • Timing profiles are representative of published descriptions (threat library), not read off any recording.
