What this report does not show

  • Only the cases listed were run; nothing is said about other threats, powers, signals or receivers.
  • Event windows are the stated times (scheduled or scripted), not measured on-air times.
  • Monitor verdicts are the open receiver-trust engine's, on the receiver's log; they are MODELLED.
  • Library cases score the open engine's synthetic NMEA receiver model on a MODELLED analogue of a published event; they show the method, not the behaviour of any product.
