Evidence mapped to NIST IR 8323r1 Foundational PNT Profile

Each duty below is mapped to the Kshana evidence products whose signed output can be put before the operator's assessor for it, and lists what Kshana does not provide. The pack is an aid for preparing an audit. It does not state, and must not be read as stating, that any system or operator meets the framework; that judgement belongs to the operator, its assessor and its authority.

Source: Foundational PNT Profile: Applying the Cybersecurity Framework for the Responsible Use of PNT Services (NIST IR 8323r1, January 2023), https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ir/2023/NIST.IR.8323r1.pdf. As recorded by the open engine's mapping: Profile tables 3 to 25 read for the subcategories cited; identifiers follow the Profile's tables (Cybersecurity Framework 1.1).
