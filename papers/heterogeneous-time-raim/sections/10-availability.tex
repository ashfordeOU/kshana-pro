\label{sec:availability}
The reference substrate for this paper is part of the open-source \code{kshana}
engine \citep{baweja2026kshana} (\url{https://github.com/ashfordeOU/kshana};
AGPL-3.0-only). The heterogeneous $\utck{}$ traceability-bias budget of
Sections~\ref{sec:hetero}--\ref{sec:corr}---the overbound
reconstruction~\eqref{eq:overbound}, the bias cross-covariance~\eqref{eq:sigmab}
with its positive-semidefiniteness (Proposition~\ref{prop:psd}), and the
correlated-$\ge$-independent inequality~\eqref{eq:corrgeqindep}
(Theorem~\ref{thm:corr})---is in \code{src/integrity/hetero\_budget.rs}; the
generalized-least-squares common-mode layer of Sections~\ref{sec:gls}--\ref{sec:blind}---the
Cholesky whitening~\eqref{eq:whiten}, the common-mode statistic~\eqref{eq:cmstat},
the post-fit vanishing~\eqref{eq:postfitzero}, and the finite-ceiling
bound~\eqref{eq:ceiling}--\eqref{eq:blindconds} (Theorem~\ref{thm:finite})---is in
\code{src/integrity/gls\_commonmode.rs}. These compose the engine's already-tested
rank-one fusion, solution-separation, and clock-state primitives; no result is
hard-coded in a print statement.

The demonstration of Section~\ref{sec:demo}---the correlated fused bias
$\SI{7.788}{\nano\second}$ against the naive-independent $\SI{6.681}{\nano\second}$,
the common-mode statistic $0.01\to0.68$, and the finite ceiling $2.371$---is
regenerated by the committed example
\begin{quote}
  \code{cargo run -{}-example hetero\_gls\_demo}
\end{quote}
which builds the five heterogeneous sources with sources $0$ and $1$ sharing the
$\utc{}(\mathrm{USNO})$ realizer, fuses the correlated and independent biases,
whitens the residual, evaluates the common-mode statistic on the null and shifted
cases, and prints the undetectable ceiling. The \Proven{} structural
properties---the correlated-$\ge$-independent inequality, its single-realizer
$\rho\!\to\!1$ linear reduction, the post-fit vanishing of the common-mode
statistic, and the mutual-exclusivity finite-ceiling bound for every nonzero
direction---are each asserted by unit tests in the two modules
(\code{correlated\_fused\_bias\_dominates\_independent\_when\_correlated},
\code{rho\_one\_single\_realizer\_recovers\_linear\_sum},
\code{post\_fit\_common\_mode\_statistic\_is\_zero},
\code{every\_nonzero\_direction\_under\_pd\_omega\_has\_a\_finite\_ceiling}); the
value of the common-mode statistic and the whitening quadratic forms are
cross-checked against independent \code{numpy} computations by
\code{gls\_matches\_numpy\_reference} and \code{algebra\_matches\_numpy\_reference}.
The $\chi^2_1$ null distribution of the common-mode statistic is a \Proven{}
\emph{derived} property (score $=$ Wald $=$ maximum-likelihood statistic for a
common-mode shift), not a distributional unit test.

\paragraph{Verification matrix (all \Modelled{}).}
Every row of the demonstration is a \Modelled{} scenario over \Proven{} algebra;
\emph{none} is externally \Validated{}. The correlated-bias inequality, the
$\Sib{}$ positive-semidefiniteness, the common-mode statistic (its \emph{value}
machine-checked against \code{numpy}; its $\chi^2_1$ null distribution a \Proven{}
derived property, not a distributional unit test), the post-fit vanishing, and the
finite-ceiling bound are \Proven{}; the per-source $U_i$, the tail allocation, and
the correlation $\rho$ are \Cited{} magnitudes and \Modelled{} choices; the
demonstration magnitudes are \Modelled{} illustration. The BIPM Circular-T $[\utck{}-\utck{}]$ series
\citep{bipmcirculart} used to size the biases is the companion paper's
\Validated{} oracle and is a \Cited{} input here---reproducing its values is
bookkeeping, not validation (Section~\ref{sec:datasetnote}). This paper adds no new
\Validated{} row.

\paragraph{Availability status.}
The two modules and the example named above are part of the \code{kshana} reference
substrate; their public push and the corresponding tagged release are pending at
the time of writing, so no permalink to a specific commit is given here. A
productized operational timing-integrity capability, as opposed to the open
reference substrate that reproduces this paper's numbers, is a commercial capability
of \code{kshana-pro} and is not part of this open release.
