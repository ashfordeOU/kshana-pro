\section{Limitations}

A few things this study is not. It is closed-loop: truth is generated from the
same observation model it is then recovered through. That makes it an
observability study, not a validation against real VLBI or ranging data; it
bounds what the geometry can determine under the stated noise, not what a real
campaign will get. It is also a snapshot, geometric fit. There is no force-model
propagation in the solver, no dynamic arc or unmodelled forces, no real ephemeris
or station-coordinate errors, and no troposphere, ionosphere or relativistic
delay modelling beyond what the observable already carries; Earth-orientation
polar motion is set to zero. The joint-OD result is Modelled, against an
internal-consistency oracle, and only the reused DOP and Earth-orientation
kernels are externally validated.

The constellation and station parameters are illustrative and drawn from public
sources, so the engine models the class of system rather than any specific one.
It is not Moonlight, LCNS, NovaMoon, or LunaNet, and it is not affiliated with or
endorsed by ESA. There is no TRL above 3, no flight heritage, and no
certification.
