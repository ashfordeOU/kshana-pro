\section{Related work}

Lunar navigation architecture and LCNS signal/service studies; deep-space
radiometric tracking and $\Delta$-DOR \citep{giorgini1996horizons}; lunar
reference-frame and Coordinated-Lunar-Time definition \citep{petit2010iers}; and
open PNT/OD tooling (GMAT \citep{nasa_gmat}, Orekit \citep{orekit}, RTKLIB
\citep{takasu2009}; the LuPNT lunar simulator \citep{iiyama2023lupnt}; the
kshana engine itself \citep{baweja2026kshana}). Our contribution is not a new
estimator but an \textbf{open, reproducible observability finding} for the lunar
joint-OD problem, with the information-not-count control that distinguishes a
geometric observability claim from a data-volume effect.
