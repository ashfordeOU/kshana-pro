\label{sec:availability}
The reference substrate for this paper is part of the open-source \code{kshana}
engine \citep{baweja2026kshana} (\url{https://github.com/ashfordeOU/kshana};
AGPL-3.0-only). The scalar timing protection level of Section~\ref{sec:scalar}
(the rank-one MHSS specialization, the fused variance \eqref{eq:fused}, the
separation identity \eqref{eq:sepvar}, and the $N=1$ closed form \eqref{eq:n1}) is
in \code{src/integrity/tpl\_scalar.rs}; the integrity multipliers, including the
running-max $\Kf(\IR/2)=\Phi^{-1}(1-\IR/4)$ of \eqref{eq:kir}, are in
\code{src/integrity/kir.rs}; the $\Pzero$-seeded holdover envelope \eqref{eq:hpl}
and the composed ride-through PL \eqref{eq:composed}, together with the
machine-checkable lower-semicontinuity predicate of Theorem~\ref{thm:lsc}, are in
\code{src/integrity/composed\_pl.rs}; and the law-of-the-iterated-logarithm
envelope of Section~\ref{sec:lil} is in \code{src/integrity/lil\_envelope.rs}.
These compose the engine's already-tested \code{raim} (MHSS), \code{holdover}
(coast phase variance), and \code{clock\_state} primitives; no result is
hard-coded in a print statement.

The representative ride-through of Table~\ref{tab:ridethrough}---the
\SI{26.961}{\nano\second} handover TPL and the day- and week-scale composed
PL---is regenerated by the committed example
\begin{quote}
  \code{cargo run -{}-example cti\_p1\_composed\_pl}
\end{quote}
which builds the three heterogeneous sources, computes the scalar TPL at handover,
seeds the coast from a disciplined-clock handover covariance, and prints
$\PL(\tau)=\max(\TPL_{\mathrm{handover}},\HPL(\tau))$ over the coast grid. The
\Proven{} structural properties---the bias-driven scalar PL, the exponent lint,
the running-max multiplier, the $\Pzero$-seed safety, the lower-semicontinuity, and
the LIL impossibility/consistency---are each asserted by unit tests in the
respective modules.

The single \Validated{} row---the holdover-leg running-max envelope
overbound-covering the real BIPM $[\utck{}-\mathrm{UTC(USNO)}]$ series on the
disjoint multi-year regime ($\tau\ge\SI{90}{\day}$)---is reproduced end to end by
\begin{quote}
  \code{python3 scripts/gen\_cti\_holdover\_coverage.py}\\
  \code{cargo test -{}-test cti\_holdover\_coverage\_reference}
\end{quote}
The \code{numpy}/\code{scipy} script fits the white-FM diffusion on the early
segment ($\mathrm{MJD}<58000$), forms the $\Kf(\IR/2)$ running-max envelope at
$\IR=\num{e-2}$, computes the running-max excursion of the real series on the
disjoint late segment ($\mathrm{MJD}\ge58000$), and writes
\code{tests/fixtures/cti/reference.json} ($q_{\mathrm{wf}}=\SI{7.533e-25}{\square\second\per\second}$,
$\Kf(\IR/2)=2.807$, and the coverage table); the Rust test reconstructs the
envelope independently and fails the build on any mismatch or on any multi-year
piercing. The oracle series
(\code{tests/fixtures/cti/utc\_utcusno.csv}) is the BIPM Circular-T five-day
$[\utck{}-\mathrm{UTC(USNO)}]$ data, MJD 56074--60429 (872 points), retrieved from
the BIPM Time Department \code{webtai} API; the data are © BIPM and freely
distributed via Circular-T. The vendored provenance and honesty scope---what is
validated (multi-year overbound coverage), what is a disclosed \Modelled{} short-lag
boundary ($\tau\le\SI{30}{\day}$), and what stays \Modelled{} (tightness,
free-running coast, the deep tail, and the composition)---are recorded in
\code{tests/fixtures/cti/NOTICE.md}. A productized operational timing-integrity
capability, as opposed to the open reference substrate that reproduces this paper's
numbers, is a commercial capability of \code{kshana-pro} and is not part of this
open release.
