\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Position against the closest prior art.}
The contribution is a composition, and it is worth stating exactly what each
neighbouring result already owns. Gioia's receiver-autonomous timing protection
level \citep{gioia2023} is a genuine single-regime GNSS TPL; it does not compose
with holdover and does not carry a heterogeneous budget, and we cite it as the
GNSS-TPL parent rather than surpass it on its own ground. IEEE~802.1ASed
\citep{ieee8021ased} standardizes fault-tolerant \emph{selection}---rejecting a
lying source across redundant domains with local-oscillator holdover---but
deliberately emits no protection level or integrity-risk budget; the composed PL
is the quantitative layer that standard leaves open, and could in principle be
reported by a conformant overlay consuming the same managed objects.
IEEE~P1952 \citep{ieeep1952} specifies a qualitative holdover-drift-consistency
check at its ``Verify'' level; the $\Pzero$-seeded holdover envelope of
Section~\ref{sec:holdover} is, in effect, a closed form for that qualitative
check. And Baweja's conditional TPL \citep{baweja2026tpl} is the $N=1$
single-transition special case that Sections~\ref{sec:n1} and~\ref{sec:baweja}
recover and generalize. The MHSS algebra itself is aviation ARAIM
\citep{blanch2015,joerger2014}, cited and specialized, never reclaimed.

\paragraph{The honest novelty bound.}
Stated plainly, the novelty is three things and no more: (i) the \emph{composition}
object $\PL(\tau)=\max(\TPL_{\mathrm{handover}},\HPL(\tau))$ across the
cross-check--to--holdover seam; (ii) the \emph{modelling insight} that demanding
continuity at the seam is unsafe---because $\TPL_{\mathrm{handover}}$ carries
fault-mode overbounds a coast lacks---and the $\max(\cdot)$ remedy that removes the
downward jump, whose resulting property we \emph{name} lower-semicontinuity
$\PL(0^{+})\ge\PL(0^{-})$ (the correct, weaker-than-continuity property that makes
the seam safe), together with the $\Pzero$-seeding safety requirement; and (iii)
the single \emph{multi-year overbound-coverage} anchor of the holdover leg against
a real BIPM series. The LIL statements (Section~\ref{sec:lil}) are a sharpening of
a cited impossibility into a positive growth-order result, not a new limit
theorem. We claim these and nothing wider; in particular we make no
formally-verified, certified, or technology-readiness claim, and the word
\Validated{} appears nowhere near the composition, the heterogeneous budget, or any
end-to-end system statement, all of which are \Proven{} algebra over \Modelled{}
scenarios.

\paragraph{Limits, and what is deferred.}
Three deliberate exclusions bound this paper and set up its companions. The
\emph{heterogeneous multi-source budget}---per-source $\utck{}$ traceability biases
with correlated cross-covariance, assembled from Circular-T and CGGTTS as an
integrity-budget input---is treated only as an input here and is the subject of a
companion paper. The \emph{common-mode / generalized-least-squares} correction for
a fault shared across sources, which solution separation is structurally blind to,
is likewise deferred: the independence assumption of Section~\ref{sec:sourcemodel}
is stated, not solved. And the \emph{active delay/spoof adversary}, the
authenticated traceable timestamp protocol, and an open timing-integrity benchmark
are later papers in the arc; the delay-attack constraint that a symmetric
single-path delay is provably undetectable \citep{mizrahi7384} bounds any future
accuracy claim and is noted here only as context. The single highest-value open
anchor is empirical: a real \emph{seconds-to-hours}, free-running short-coast
oracle, the regime the multi-year Circular-T series does not inform
(Section~\ref{sec:covscope}).

\paragraph{Reading the numbers.}
Every magnitude in this paper---the \SI{9.83}{\nano\second} single-source TPL, the
\SI{26.961}{\nano\second} handover TPL, the day- and week-scale ride-through
values, and the coverage envelope heights---is a \Modelled{} number for a
representative source or a fitted real series, regenerated by one command from the
open engine. None is an operational tolerance, a certified alert limit, or an
endorsement; they illustrate the structure, and the structure is what the
\Proven{} and \Validated{} claims are about.
