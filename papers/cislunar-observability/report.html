How long an arc until the four-state is observable?

A four-DRO constellation (chief index 0) is differential-corrected to closing, retrograde orbits (max periodicity residual 5.27e-9 [Validated]). Over a 24-epoch tracking arc the observable rank grows from a single line-of-sight toward the full four-state; the Gramian condition number and eigen-spectrum are read off the finite-difference-validated open core and cross-validated against an independent SRIF whose posterior covariance turns finite exactly at the full-rank arc (1).
