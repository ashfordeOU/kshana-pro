\section{Methods}
\label{app:methods}

This appendix records the exact rules behind the results: the four-region
classifier, the coverage arithmetic, the fault generators' offset profiles, the
verdict decision table, and the \code{InternalConsistency} fixture procedure. All
are consistent with the committed modules \code{src/benchmark/*} and the vendored
provenance notice (Section~\ref{sec:availability}).

\subsection{The classifier}
\label{app:classify}

For a true error $e=|\test-\utc|$, a declared protection level $\PL$, and an alert
limit $\AL$, the classifier of \eqref{eq:classify} is evaluated in the fixed
precedence order below (the first matching branch wins):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item if $\PL>\AL$: \Unavail{} (the monitor has flagged itself; the true error
  is not consulted);
  \item else if $e\le\PL$: \Nominal{};
  \item else if $e\le\AL$: \MI{};
  \item else: \HMI{}.
\end{enumerate}
The bounds are inclusive at $e=\PL$ (\Nominal{}) and at $e=\AL$ with $e>\PL$
(\MI{}), and the sign of $\test-\utc$ is discarded before comparison, so the
protection level is a symmetric two-sided bound. The unit tests
\code{four\_regions} and \code{boundaries\_and\_sign} in \code{stanford.rs} check
every branch and both inclusive boundaries at the exact boundary values, and check
that a $\PL>\AL$ epoch is \Unavail{} regardless of $e$.

\subsection{The coverage arithmetic}
\label{app:coverage}

Over $n$ epochs with region counts
$(n_{\Nominal{}},n_{\Unavail{}},n_{\MI{}},n_{\HMI{}})$ (which sum to $n$), the
scorer forms the rates of \eqref{eq:rates}---$\mathrm{hmi\_rate}=n_{\HMI{}}/n$,
$\mathrm{mi\_rate}=n_{\MI{}}/n$, $\mathrm{availability}=1-n_{\Unavail{}}/n$
(the Stanford--WAAS usable fraction), and $\mathrm{nominal\_rate}=n_{\Nominal{}}/n$
(the tightness signal)---and the coverage boolean
$\mathrm{coverage\_ok}=[\mathrm{hmi\_rate}\le\IR]$. For $n=0$ all rates are defined
to be zero and $\mathrm{coverage\_ok}$ is true (vacuous safety). Only $n_{\HMI{}}$
enters the coverage decision; the misleading, nominal, and unavailable tallies are
reported as tightness and continuity signals but do not gate coverage. The tests \code{clean\_monitor\_covers},
\code{under\_bounded\_monitor\_fails\_coverage},
\code{declaring\_unavailable\_is\_safe}, and \code{empty\_input\_is\_vacuously\_safe}
in \code{coverage.rs} pin the four qualitative cases.

\subsection{The fault generators}
\label{app:faults}

Each generator emits a length-$n$ true-error series $e(k)$ and a \code{FaultSpec}
$\{\text{class},\text{detectability},\text{max\_offset}\}$, with $\text{max\_offset}
=\max_k|e(k)|$. The offset profiles are, for epoch index $k=0,\dots,n-1$:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{l l}
  \toprule
  generator & $e(k)$ \\
  \midrule
  \code{static\_delay}$(n,o)$              & $o$ \\
  \code{incremental\_delay}$(n,r)$         & $r\,k$ \\
  \code{domain\_divergence}$(n,r)$         & $r\,k$ \\
  \code{clock\_slam}$(n,s,k_0)$            & $s\,\mathbf 1[k\ge k_0]$ \\
  \code{holdover\_coast}$(n,d,r_{\mathrm{rw}})$ & $\tfrac12 d\,k^{2}+r_{\mathrm{rw}}\,k$ \\
  \code{symmetric\_delay}$(n,o)$           & $o$ \quad(\code{Undetectable}) \\
  \code{asymmetric\_delay}$(n,\Delta)$     & $\tfrac12\Delta$ \\
  \code{replay\_within\_freshness}$(n,o)$  & $o$ \quad(\code{Undetectable}) \\
  \code{path\_selective}$(n,o,f)$          & $o\,f$ \\
  \code{k\_of\_n\_quorum}$(n,o,k,N)$       & $o\,(k/N)$, or $0$ if $N=0$ \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
All classes are \code{Detectable} except \code{symmetric\_delay} and
\code{replay\_within\_freshness}, which are \code{Undetectable} \citep{mizrahi7384};
the asymmetric-delay offset is exactly half the path asymmetry \citep{narula2018}.
The default \code{full\_menu} instantiates all ten with $n=64$ and contains exactly
two \code{Undetectable} scenarios; the tests \code{offset\_profiles},
\code{undetectable\_faults\_are\_flagged}, and \code{max\_offset\_matches\_series}
in \code{faults.rs} check the profiles, the flags, the half-asymmetry offset, the
$\text{max\_offset}$ consistency, and the count of exactly two undetectable classes.

\subsection{The verdict decision table}
\label{app:verdict}

The scorecard runs a monitor $\PL(\cdot)$ over each scenario and emits one of the
four verdicts of \eqref{eq:verdict} by the table below; there is no fifth
(detection) verdict, and the \code{Undetectable} row can only reach an absorption
verdict.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{l l l}
  \toprule
  detectability & condition & verdict \\
  \midrule
  \code{Detectable}   & $\mathrm{hmi\_rate}\le\IR$        & \CovPass{} \\
  \code{Detectable}   & $\mathrm{hmi\_rate}>\IR$          & \CovFail{} \\
  \code{Undetectable} & $\PL(k)\ge|e(k)|\ \forall k$      & \Absorbed{} \\
  \code{Undetectable} & $\exists\,k:\ \PL(k)<|e(k)|$      & \Unabsorbed{} \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The aggregate scorecard sums $n_{\HMI{}}^{\text{total}}$ over all scenarios and
sets $\mathrm{all\_undetectable\_absorbed}$ true iff every \code{Undetectable}
scenario earned \Absorbed{}. The tests
\code{reference\_pl\_absorbs\_undetectable},
\code{broken\_pl\_fails\_to\_absorb\_but\_never\_detects},
\code{detectable\_fault\_uses\_coverage\_verdict}, and
\code{undetectable\_never\_yields\_a\_coverage\_verdict} in \code{scorecard.rs}
check each row and the impossibility invariant of Theorem~\ref{thm:invariant}
across protection levels spanning zero to $10^{6}$.

\subsection{The \code{InternalConsistency} fixture procedure}
\label{app:fixture}

The scorer's arithmetic is cross-checked, not validated, by an independent
\code{numpy} re-count (Section~\ref{sec:oracle}):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \code{scripts/gen\_tib\_scorer\_reference.py} generates a fixed synthetic
  set of $(e,\PL)$ pairs at a fixed $\AL$ and stated $\IR$, classifies each by the
  four-region rule of Appendix~\ref{app:classify}, and counts the regions.
  \item It writes \code{tests/fixtures/tib/reference.json}: the inputs, the counts
  ($n=80$; $n_{\Nominal{}}=40$, $n_{\MI{}}=20$, $n_{\HMI{}}=10$,
  $n_{\Unavail{}}=10$), the rates ($\mathrm{hmi\_rate}=0.125$,
  $\mathrm{mi\_rate}=0.25$, $\mathrm{availability}=0.875$,
  $\mathrm{nominal\_rate}=0.5$), and
  $\mathrm{coverage\_ok}=\text{false}$ (since $0.125>\IR$).
  \item \code{tests/tib\_scorer\_reference.rs} reads the fixture, runs the Rust
  scorer on the identical inputs, and asserts every count matches exactly and every
  rate matches to \num{e-12}.
\end{enumerate}
This checks that two independent implementations of the \emph{counting} agree; it
asserts nothing about the accuracy of any monitor, and is therefore \Modelled{}~/~
\code{InternalConsistency}, never \Validated{}, as the fixture's \code{NOTICE.md}
states.
