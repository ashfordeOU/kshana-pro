\section*{Acknowledgements}
The scoring machinery, fault catalogue, verdict algebra, and end-to-end
demonstration underlying this work are part of the open-source \code{kshana}
engine. The scoring taxonomy is the Stanford--ESA integrity diagram; the
undetectable-fault set is the delay-attack impossibility of RFC~7384. No external
dataset underwrites any accuracy claim, and by design none is required: a
benchmark asserts nothing about accuracy. This research received no specific
grant from any funding agency in the public, commercial, or not-for-profit
sectors.
