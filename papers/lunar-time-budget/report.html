The single-τ artifact, answered

For the passive-h-maser clock the seven LTC error terms are assembled as time-error curves x_i(τ) over 57 averaging times and root-summed into x_Σ(τ). The growing clock term overtakes the constant frame-realisation term at the crossover τ* = 8.689e4 s (flicker-floor) — below it the budget is frame-limited, above it clock-limited. The crossover matches its closed form to a residual of 0.0e0 [Validated]. The frame term is 1.001 ns (δr = 0.3 m).
