Does layering buy resilience — and does it survive correlation?

A 4-layer PNT architecture is stressed by a seeded Monte-Carlo threat sweep. The layered resilience ratio lands at closed-form 6.73x (MC 6.23x) under the INDEPENDENCE assumption; as the shared-vector denial correlation rises it collapses toward 1 (correlation defeats layering).
