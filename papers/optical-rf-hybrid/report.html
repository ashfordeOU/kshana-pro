Precision, integrity, availability

The 1550 nm (two-way) optical link spreads to a 700.2 m footprint at range (≈ (λ/D)·L, Validated), delivering 1.01e9 detected photons and a photon-limited ranging bound far below the RF code-tracking jitter. The inverse-variance hybrid inherits the tighter optical precision while the RF link supplies the always-on integrity and availability. Cross-modality RAIM reports HPL 5.17e0 m / TPL 1.22e-8 s (fault_detected = false), and the optical↔RF handoff is mean-continuous = true (Validated no-jump invariant).
