\label{sec:availability}
The datum-identifiability reference substrate is part of the open-source
\code{kshana} engine \citep{baweja2026kshana}
(\url{https://github.com/ashfordeOU/kshana}; AGPL-3.0-only). The seven-parameter
datum partials, the Schur decomposition, the multi-technique combiner, and the
representative measurement menu are in the open modules \code{lunar\_datum},
\code{lunar\_identifiability}, and \code{lunar\_techniques}. The budget-constrained
experiment-design optimizer that consumes the menu---the greedy/exact subset
selection and the cost/degeneracy Pareto frontier---is a commercial capability in
the \code{kshana-pro} engine, not part of the open crate. Every number of the open
reference is regenerated by
\begin{quote}
  \code{cargo run -{}-example p1\_datum\_identifiability}
\end{quote}
which prints the single-observation defect, the LLR-only baseline
$(\mu,\corr,c_x)$, the marginal gains and their ratio, and the per-parameter CRLB
fractions; the cost/degeneracy Pareto frontier (Part~C) is produced by the
\code{kshana-pro} optimizer. The independent SciPy/NumPy oracle is
\code{scripts/gen\_datum\_identifiability\_ref.py}, its output vendored with a
provenance notice and SHA-256 at \code{tests/fixtures/datum\_identifiability/}. The
DE440 orientation series and the retroreflector coordinates are vendored with
notices at \code{tests/fixtures/llr\_geometry/}, which also records the
representative (\Modelled) menu beacons, orbit, noise, and costs. All classification
and decomposition claims are asserted in the continuous-integration test suite.
