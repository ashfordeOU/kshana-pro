# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (Template)

**⚠️ DRAFT TEMPLATE (PR4) — not legal advice. Have counsel review and adapt to the
governing jurisdiction before signing.** Use this for **consortium / bid
discussions** (e.g. an ESA AO/1-13503 consortium) where both parties exchange
confidential information.

---

**Parties.** This Agreement is made on **[DATE]** between **Ashforde OÜ**
[registered address, Estonia] and **[OTHER PARTY LEGAL NAME]** [registered address]
(each a "Party"; together the "Parties").

**1. Purpose.** Mutual evaluation of a potential collaboration, including a joint
response to **[tender / AO reference, e.g. ESA AO/1-13503 Quantum-Enabled PNT
Demonstrator]** and the scoping of respective work packages (the "Purpose").

**2. Confidential Information.** Any non-public information disclosed by either Party
("Disclosing Party") to the other ("Receiving Party"), in any form, including
technical approaches, performance data, pricing, work-package scoping, and bid
strategy, whether or not marked confidential.

**3. Obligations.** Each Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely
for the Purpose, protect it with no less than reasonable care, limit disclosure to
personnel/advisers with a need to know who are bound by equivalent obligations, and
not use it to compete against the Disclosing Party outside the Purpose.

**4. Exclusions.** As in standard practice: public-domain, prior knowledge,
independent development, or rightful third-party receipt without restriction.

**5. Compelled disclosure.** Permitted if legally compelled, with prompt notice where
lawful.

**6. No licence; IP ownership.** No licence or assignment of IP is granted. Each
Party retains all rights in its **Background IPR** (for Ashforde, the open Kshana
engine and the Kshana Pro framework). Ownership of any **Foreground** created in a
subsequent collaboration is governed by a separate consortium/contract agreement and,
where applicable, the funder's IPR terms (e.g. ESA R&D IPR: Background retained;
Foreground owned by the creating contractor with funder usage rights).

**7. No obligation; no partnership.** This Agreement does not oblige either Party to
proceed with the collaboration or bid, nor does it create a partnership, agency, or
exclusive relationship.

**8. Term & return.** Obligations survive **[3–5] years** from disclosure; return or
destruction on request, subject to standard archival/backup carve-outs.

**9. Governing law & disputes.** [Mutually agreed], courts of [venue].

**10. Miscellaneous.** Entire agreement; written amendments; no assignment without
consent; severability; counterparts.
