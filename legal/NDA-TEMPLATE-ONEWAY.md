# One-Way Non-Disclosure Agreement (Template)

**⚠️ DRAFT TEMPLATE (PR4) — not legal advice. Have counsel review and adapt to the
governing jurisdiction before signing.** Use this when **Ashforde receives**
confidential information (e.g. a hardware partner's device characterisation data)
and is the sole receiving party.

---

**Parties.** This Agreement is made on **[DATE]** between:

- **Discloser:** [PARTNER LEGAL NAME], [registered address] ("Discloser"); and
- **Recipient:** **Ashforde OÜ**, [registered address, Estonia] ("Recipient").

**1. Purpose.** Evaluation and development of a device-specific performance model
("digital twin") of the Discloser's [DEVICE — e.g. cold-atom interferometer / optical
clock] within the Kshana Pro framework (the "Purpose").

**2. Confidential Information.** Any non-public information disclosed by the
Discloser, in any form, including device characterisation data, calibration sets,
test results, designs, and specifications, whether or not marked confidential.

**3. Obligations.** Recipient shall: (a) use the Confidential Information solely for
the Purpose; (b) protect it with at least the care it uses for its own confidential
information and no less than reasonable care; (c) disclose it only to personnel with
a need to know who are bound by equivalent obligations; (d) not reverse-engineer
beyond what the Purpose requires.

**4. Exclusions.** Obligations do not apply to information that is/becomes public
through no fault of Recipient, was lawfully known before disclosure, is independently
developed without use of the Confidential Information, or is rightfully received from
a third party without restriction.

**5. Compelled disclosure.** Recipient may disclose if legally compelled, giving
prompt notice (where lawful) so the Discloser may seek protective measures.

**6. No licence; ownership.** No licence to any IP is granted except the limited
right to use the Confidential Information for the Purpose. **Foreground created by
Recipient using the Confidential Information** (e.g. a fitted twin, methodology) is
addressed in the separate development/engagement agreement; absent that, this NDA
grants the Discloser no rights in Recipient's pre-existing IP (the Kshana engine and
Pro framework remain Recipient's Background IPR).

**7. Term & return.** Confidentiality obligations survive **[3–5] years** from
disclosure. On request, Recipient returns or destroys the Confidential Information,
except one archival copy for compliance and any copies in routine backups.

**8. No warranty.** Confidential Information is provided "as is".

**9. Governing law & disputes.** [Estonia / mutually agreed], courts of [venue].

**10. Miscellaneous.** Entire agreement on its subject; amendments in writing; no
assignment without consent; severability.
