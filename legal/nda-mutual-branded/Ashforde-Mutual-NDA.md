# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

2026-10-04 · Prepared by Ashforde

## Parties and purpose

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of [Effective Date] (the "Effective Date") between:

- **Ashforde OÜ**, a private limited company registered in Estonia, registry code [registry code], with its registered office at [address] ("Ashforde"); and
- **[Counterparty legal name]**, a [entity type] organised under the laws of [jurisdiction], with its principal place of business at [address] (the "Counterparty").

Each is a "Party" and together the "Parties." In relation to any given information, the Party disclosing it is the "Discloser" and the Party receiving it is the "Recipient." This Agreement is mutual: each Party may act in either role.

**Purpose.** The Parties wish to explore a possible technical and commercial relationship concerning the evaluation of Ashforde's GNSS receiver and signal-analysis software against the Counterparty's receivers, simulators, recordings, test conditions and data (the "Purpose"). To pursue the Purpose, each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other.

## 1. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by the Discloser to the Recipient, in any form (oral, written, electronic, visual or by access to systems or samples), that is marked or identified as confidential, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure.

It includes, without limitation: software, source code, algorithms, models and methods; technical data, IQ recordings, measurements, test conditions and results; roadmaps, designs and know-how; business, commercial, financial and customer information; and the existence and content of the Parties' discussions. It also includes information of a third party that the Discloser is obliged to keep confidential.

Confidential Information does not include software or materials that a Party has made publicly available under an open-source licence. In particular, the publicly released Kshana engine is licensed to everyone under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0); it is not confidential, and nothing in this Agreement changes, restricts or adds conditions to the rights anyone has under that licence. Confidentiality under this Agreement attaches only to a Party's non-public materials, such as unreleased code, pre-release builds, Ashforde's proprietary (non-open-source) components, roadmaps not yet published, and the Counterparty's data and test information.

## 2. Obligations of the Recipient

The Recipient shall:

1. use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
2. hold it in confidence and not disclose it to any third party without the Discloser's prior written consent;
3. protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of like importance, and in no event less than reasonable care; and
4. disclose it only to its directors, employees, affiliates and professional advisers who need it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement (its "Representatives"). The Recipient is responsible for any breach by its Representatives.

The Recipient shall not reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any software, sample or materials provided, except to the extent that restriction is prohibited by applicable law.

The Recipient shall not use the Confidential Information to develop, or to help any third party develop, a product or service that competes with the Discloser's, and shall not use it other than for the Purpose. Any use of the Confidential Information is at the Recipient's own risk.

## 3. Exclusions

The obligations in this Agreement do not apply to information that the Recipient can show:

1. was or became publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by the Recipient or its Representatives;
2. was known to the Recipient without a duty of confidentiality before the Discloser disclosed it;
3. was independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information; or
4. was rightfully received from a third party free to disclose it.

## 4. Compelled disclosure

The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation or a valid court or governmental order. Where lawful and practicable, the Recipient shall give the Discloser prompt prior written notice so the Discloser may seek a protective order or other remedy, disclose only the portion legally required, and use reasonable efforts to ensure it is treated confidentially.

## 5. Ownership, reservation of rights and feedback

1. **Ownership.** All Confidential Information, and all intellectual property rights in it, remain the sole property of the Discloser. This includes Ashforde's software, source code, object code, pre-release and evaluation builds, algorithms, models, architecture, methods and roadmap, and the Counterparty's receivers, simulators, recordings, test conditions, measurements and results.
2. **No licence beyond the Purpose.** Disclosure grants the Recipient no licence or other right in the Discloser's Confidential Information or intellectual property, by implication, estoppel or otherwise, except a limited, revocable, non-transferable right to use it internally for the Purpose.
3. **No residual rights.** This Agreement grants no "residuals" licence. Nothing here gives the Recipient any right to use Confidential Information that its personnel may retain in unaided memory.
4. **Feedback.** If the Recipient gives the Discloser comments or suggestions about the Discloser's products or Confidential Information ("Feedback"), the Discloser may use that Feedback without restriction or obligation, and the Recipient grants the Discloser a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to do so.
5. **Independent development.** Nothing in this Agreement limits either Party from independently conceiving, developing, acquiring, marketing or continuing its own products, technology or roadmap, so long as it does so without use of the other Party's Confidential Information. This Agreement does not require either Party to restrict the work or assignments of any of its personnel.

**Open-source rights preserved.** Nothing in this Agreement limits or adds conditions to any right a Party has under an open-source licence, including the AGPL-3.0 under which the public Kshana engine is released. Where this Agreement and such a licence conflict as to publicly licensed code, that licence prevails.

## 6. Evaluation software and materials

Where Ashforde provides the Counterparty with software, builds, outputs or other materials for evaluation (the "Evaluation Materials"), the following apply in addition to the terms above:

1. the Evaluation Materials are licensed, not sold, solely for the Counterparty's internal evaluation for the Purpose and for the evaluation period the Parties agree;
2. the Counterparty shall not distribute, sublicense, rent, host as a service, or otherwise provide the Evaluation Materials to any third party, and shall not use them in production or for any commercial purpose;
3. the Counterparty shall not reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any non-public component, except to the extent that restriction is prohibited by applicable law, and shall not remove any proprietary notice;
4. the Counterparty shall not publish or disclose any benchmark, performance or comparison result relating to the Evaluation Materials without Ashforde's prior written consent;
5. the Evaluation Materials are provided "as is"; title to them and all intellectual property in them remains with Ashforde; and
6. on request or at the end of the evaluation, the Counterparty shall stop using, and shall return or destroy, the Evaluation Materials.

This section applies only to Ashforde's non-public, proprietary and pre-release materials. Any component provided under an open-source licence, including code under the AGPL-3.0, remains governed solely by that licence; nothing in this section limits the rights it grants, and where it and this section conflict as to that component, the open-source licence prevails.

## 7. Third-party and restricted information

1. Each Party shall disclose only information it is free to disclose, and shall not disclose to the other any information it is barred from sharing by law or by agreement with a third party.
2. The Parties acknowledge that some of the Counterparty's data and materials may be subject to obligations owed to third parties, including a European company and the European Space Agency (ESA). The Counterparty shall not disclose any such information to Ashforde unless it is permitted to do so, and shall identify any restriction that applies to information it does share.
3. Neither Party is obliged to disclose any particular information. If restricted information is disclosed in error, the Recipient shall, on notice, treat it as Confidential Information and return or destroy it.

## 8. Export control and sanctions

Each Party shall comply with all applicable export control and sanctions laws, including European Union dual-use regulations and United States export regulations, in disclosing and handling Confidential Information. Neither Party shall export, re-export or transfer any controlled technical data received under this Agreement except as those laws permit. A Party shall tell the other if any Confidential Information it discloses is export-controlled and, where known, state its classification.

## 9. Data protection

If any Confidential Information contains personal data, the Recipient shall process it only as necessary for the Purpose and in accordance with applicable data protection law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and shall apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards. The Parties will enter a separate data processing agreement if one is required.

## 10. Term and survival

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for [two (2)] years, unless extended or ended earlier by either Party on [thirty (30)] days' written notice. Either Party may stop disclosing Confidential Information at any time.

The Recipient's confidentiality obligations survive termination and continue for [three (3)] years from the date each item of Confidential Information was disclosed. Information that qualifies as a trade secret remains protected for as long as it qualifies as a trade secret under applicable law.

## 11. Return or destruction

On the Discloser's written request or on termination, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information and any copies, and on request confirm in writing that it has done so. The Recipient may keep one copy for legal or compliance purposes, and copies held in routine electronic backups, provided such retained copies remain subject to this Agreement.

## 12. Reservation of rights; no warranty; no obligation to proceed

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. This Agreement grants no licence or other right, by implication or otherwise, in any Confidential Information or intellectual property, except the limited right to use it for the Purpose.

Confidential Information is provided "as is." Neither Party makes any warranty as to its accuracy or completeness, and neither Party is liable for the other's reliance on it.

This Agreement does not oblige either Party to disclose any information, to proceed with the Purpose, or to enter into any further agreement. Any such commitment, if made, will be in a separate signed agreement.

## 13. Publicity

Neither Party shall use the other's name, logos or trademarks, or disclose the existence or content of the Parties' discussions or of this Agreement, in any public statement, marketing or customer reference, without the other's prior written consent. This does not prevent a disclosure permitted under Section 4.

## 14. Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the other irreparable harm for which money damages alone would be inadequate. The non-breaching Party is therefore entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief, without the need to post a bond where permitted by law, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

## 15. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [governing law jurisdiction], without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The Parties submit to the [exclusive / non-exclusive] jurisdiction of the courts of [venue] for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

## 16. General

1. **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses above or to an email address a Party designates; email notice is effective on confirmed delivery.
2. **No assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except to a successor of all or substantially all of its business.
3. **No partnership.** Nothing here creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the Parties.
4. **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes any prior understanding on it.
5. **Amendment and waiver.** Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties. A failure to enforce a right is not a waiver of it.
6. **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the rest remains in effect and the provision is limited only as far as necessary.
7. **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and together one instrument.
