## 1. What to register
- **Mark (wordmark):** `KSHANA` (standard characters — broadest protection;
  protects the name regardless of styling).
- **Optional later:** a figurative/logo mark once the visual identity is locked.
  Start with the wordmark.
- **Owner of record:** **Ashforde OÜ** (not the individual) — keeps the mark with
  the entity that licenses it.

## 2. Where to file (recommended sequence)
1. **EUIPO (EU Trade Mark)** — primary. One filing covers all EU member states;
   aligns with the EU market, ESA/EU customers, and the DE operative nationality.
2. **UK (UKIPO)** — separate post-Brexit; file if UK customers/partners matter.
3. **US (USPTO)** — file if/when US distribution or customers are real (US is
   use-based; intent-to-use (1(b)) is possible but adds cost/complexity).
4. **WIPO Madrid Protocol** — consider later to extend an EU base registration to
   additional countries efficiently, rather than filing each nationally now.

> Lead with EUIPO; add UK/US only as commercial need is real. Avoid over-filing
> pre-revenue.

## 3. Classes (Nice Classification)
- **Class 9** — downloadable/recorded software: "computer software for navigation,
  positioning, timing (PNT) simulation and resilience analysis; quantum-sensor

  software for PNT simulation and resilience analysis; scientific and technological
  research and design in the field of navigation, positioning, and timing; design
  and development of computer software."
