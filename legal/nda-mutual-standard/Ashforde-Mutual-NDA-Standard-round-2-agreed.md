Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement
Between Ashforde OÜ and the Counterparty
Effective on the date of the last signature      Prepared by Ashforde OÜ
Parties and purpose
This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of the date of the last signature below (the "Effective Date") between:
Ashforde OÜ, a private limited company registered in Estonia, registry code 17321180, with its registered office at Ahtri tn 12, 15551 Tallinn, Estonia ("Ashforde"); and
SJAWS Technologies Inc., a corporation organised under the laws of Canada (Canada Business Corporations Act, corporation number 1464649-5), with its registered office at 3200 - 650 West Georgia Street, Vancouver, British Columbia V6B 4P7, Canada (the "Counterparty").
Each is a "Party" and together the "Parties." In relation to any given information, the Party disclosing it is the "Discloser" and the Party receiving it is the "Recipient." This Agreement is mutual: each Party may act in either role.
Purpose. The Parties wish to explore a possible technical and commercial relationship concerning the evaluation of Ashforde's GNSS receiver and signal-analysis software against the Counterparty's receivers, simulators, recordings, test conditions and data (the "Purpose"). To pursue the Purpose, each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other.
1. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by the Discloser to the Recipient, in any form (oral, written, electronic, visual or by access to systems or samples), that is marked or identified as confidential, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure.
It includes, without limitation: software, source code, algorithms, models and methods; technical data, IQ recordings, measurements, test conditions and results; roadmaps, designs and know-how; business, commercial, financial and customer information; and the existence and content of the Parties' discussions. It also includes information of a third party that the Discloser is obliged to keep confidential.
Confidential Information does not include software or materials that a Party has made publicly available under an open-source licence. In particular, the publicly released Kshana engine is licensed to everyone under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0); it is not confidential, and nothing in this Agreement changes, restricts or adds conditions to the rights anyone has under that licence. Confidentiality under this Agreement attaches only to a Party's non-public materials, such as unreleased code, pre-release builds, Ashforde's proprietary (non-open-source) components, roadmaps not yet published, and the Counterparty's data and test information, methods and software code in relation to the Counterparty’s Skysweeper system.
2. Obligations of the Recipient
The Recipient shall:
(a)	use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
(b)	hold it in confidence and not disclose it to any third party without the Discloser's prior written consent;
(c)	protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of like importance, and in no event less than reasonable care; and
(d)	disclose it only to its directors, employees, affiliates and professional advisers who need it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement (its "Representatives"). The Recipient is responsible for any breach by its Representatives.
3. Exclusions
The obligations in this Agreement do not apply to information that the Recipient can show:
(a)	was or became publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by the Recipient or its Representatives;
(b)	was known to the Recipient without a duty of confidentiality before the Discloser disclosed it;
(c)	was independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information; or
(d)	was rightfully received from a third party free to disclose it.
4. Compelled disclosure
The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation or a valid court or governmental order. Where lawful and practicable, the Recipient shall give the Discloser prompt prior written notice so the Discloser may seek a protective order or other remedy, disclose only the portion legally required, and use reasonable efforts to ensure it is treated confidentially.
5. Ownership and no licence
(a)	Ownership. All Confidential Information, and all intellectual property rights in it, remain the sole property of the Discloser. This includes Ashforde's software, source code, object code, pre-release and evaluation builds, algorithms, models, architecture, methods and roadmap, and the Counterparty's software, source code, object code, pre-release and evaluation builds, algorithms, models, architecture, methods, roadmap, receivers, simulators, recordings, test conditions, measurements and results.
(b)	No licence beyond the Purpose. Disclosure grants the Recipient no licence or other right in the Discloser's Confidential Information or intellectual property, by implication, estoppel or otherwise, except a limited, revocable, non-transferable right to use it internally for the Purpose.
(c)	Feedback. Either Party may freely use general comments or suggestions the other offers about its products or technology ("Feedback"), provided that doing so does not disclose the other Party's Confidential Information.
(d)	Independent development. Nothing in this Agreement limits either Party from independently conceiving, developing, acquiring, marketing or continuing its own products, technology or roadmap, so long as it does so without use of the other Party's Confidential Information. This Agreement does not require either Party to restrict the work or assignments of any of its personnel.
Open-source rights preserved. Nothing in this Agreement limits or adds conditions to any right a Party has under an open-source licence, including the AGPL-3.0 under which the public Kshana engine is released. Where this Agreement and such a licence conflict as to publicly licensed code, that licence prevails.
6. Term and survival
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years, unless extended or ended earlier by either Party on thirty (30) days' written notice. Either Party may stop disclosing Confidential Information at any time.
The Recipient's confidentiality obligations survive termination and continue for three (3) years from the date each item of Confidential Information was disclosed. Information that qualifies as a trade secret remains protected for as long as it qualifies as a trade secret under applicable law.
7. Return or destruction
On the Discloser's written request or on termination, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information and any copies, and on request confirm in writing that it has done so. The Recipient may keep one copy for legal or compliance purposes, and copies held in routine electronic backups, provided such retained copies remain subject to this Agreement.
8. No warranty; no obligation to proceed
Confidential Information is provided "as is." Neither Party makes any warranty as to its accuracy or completeness, and neither Party is liable for the other's reliance on it.
This Agreement does not oblige either Party to disclose any information, to proceed with the Purpose, or to enter into any further agreement. Any such commitment, if made, will be in a separate signed agreement.
9. Publicity
Neither Party shall use the other's name, logos or trademarks, or disclose the existence or content of the Parties' discussions or of this Agreement, in any public statement, marketing or customer reference, without the other's prior written consent. This does not prevent a disclosure permitted under Section 4.
10. Remedies
Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the other irreparable harm for which money damages alone would be inadequate. The non-breaching Party is therefore entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief, without the need to post a bond where permitted by law, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.
11. Governing law and jurisdiction
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable in it, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The Parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the Province of Ontario for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement. Nothing in this Section prevents either Party from seeking injunctive or other interim relief under Section 10 in any court of competent jurisdiction.
12. General
(a)	Notices. Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses above or to the email addresses in this Section 12(a); email notice is effective on confirmed delivery. Notice email addresses: for Ashforde, contact@ashforde.org (attention: Founder); for the Counterparty, [notice email address] (attention: [signatory name], President and CEO), with a copy to its legal counsel at [counsel email address]. Either Party may change its notice email address by notice to the other. A copy sent to legal counsel is a courtesy and does not itself constitute notice.
(b)	No assignment. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except to a successor of all or substantially all of its business.
(c)	No partnership. Nothing here creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the Parties.
(d)	Entire agreement. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes any prior understanding on it.
(e)	Amendment and waiver. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties. A failure to enforce a right is not a waiver of it.
(f)	Severability. If any provision is held unenforceable, the rest remains in effect and the provision is limited only as far as necessary.
(g)	Counterparts. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and together one instrument.
