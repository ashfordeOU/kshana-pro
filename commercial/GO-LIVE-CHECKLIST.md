# Payment go-live checklist

**Owner:** Ashforde OÜ · **Status:** ready, waiting on the business bank account · **Date:** 2026-09-25

Every place the company's bank and payment details have to be entered once the business
bank account exists, in the order to do them. Each row names the exact field and where
to find it. Work top to bottom on the day the account confirmation arrives.

Abbreviations used here: IBAN (International Bank Account Number), BIC (Business
Identifier Code, also called the SWIFT code after the Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication), ISO (International Organization for Standardization), VAT (value added tax), KMKR (the Estonian
VAT number, *käibemaksukohustuslase registreerimisnumber*), ESA (European Space Agency),
esa-star (ESA's supplier registration system), esa-p (ESA's procurement and invoicing
system), SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area), e-MTA (the e-services portal of the Estonian
Tax and Customs Board).

---

## 0. Before you start

- [ ] The account is in the **company's** name, exactly `Ashforde OÜ`. The renderer
      refuses any other account holder, so a personal account can never be printed on a
      company invoice.
- [ ] Keep the bank's **account confirmation** (a letter or statement showing holder,
      IBAN and BIC) as a PDF. ESA's finance team can send a bank account back for
      clarification, and the esa-star Bank Details section accepts attachments, so have
      it ready before you submit anything.
- [ ] Decide the payment-reference scheme with the bank: free text, or an ISO 11649
      structured creditor reference (`RF` + check digits), which SEPA banks pass
      through unchanged.
- [ ] Confirm the VAT status with the accountant. The company's published legal notice
      said "Not registered for VAT" when read on 2026-09-25
      (<https://ashforde.org/legal>).

## 1. The single source: `commercial/payment-details.toml`

| Field | Value comes from |
|---|---|
| `[bank] account_holder` | Account confirmation. Must equal `[seller] legal_name`. |
| `[bank] iban` | Account confirmation. Spaces allowed. Checked with the ISO 13616 mod-97 checksum. |
| `[bank] bic` | Account confirmation (8 or 11 characters). |
| `[bank] bank_name`, `[bank] bank_address` | Account confirmation, as the bank writes them. |
| `[bank] currency` | The account's currency, for example `EUR`. Every deal must use it. |
| `[payment_reference] scheme`, `format` | Section 0 decision. `format` must contain `{invoice_number}`. |
| `[tax] vat_status`, `kmkr_number` | Accountant. `kmkr_number` only when registered. |
| `[seller] registry_check` | Re-read the identity at <https://ariregister.rik.ee/eng/company/17321180> and replace `verify` with the date. On 2026-09-25 that page was behind a browser challenge a script could not pass. |

Then run:

```sh
python3 commercial/tools/render.py check
python3 -m unittest discover -s commercial/tools -p 'test_*.py'
```

`check` must print `READY`. From then on every invoice, quotation and order form renders
without `--draft`. Prices and payment terms stay a separate founder decision
(`legal/PRICING.md`); the renderer also refuses while a deal still says `PLACEHOLDER`.

## 2. ESA: esa-star registration, Bank Details section

Source: *esa-star Registration User Manual*, issue 4.4, 04/05/2026, sections 2.1, 2.3 and
2.8, downloaded 2026-09-25 from <https://esastar-emr.sso.esa.int/Account/DownloadFile>;
overview at
<https://www.esa.int/About_Us/Business_with_ESA/How_to_do/Introduction_to_esa-star_registration>.

Why it matters: the manual says a bank account validated through esa-star is "a
necessary pre-requisite" for submitting invoices through esa-p after a contract award,
and for ESA to pay. The internal tender trackers list this as the open item "full
registration (bank + financials) before award" (`docs/tenders/cross-tender/kaf-doc-admin-checklist.md`).

| Step | Where in esa-star | Exact field or action |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Questionnaire → **Contacts** | Make sure a contact holds the **Bank Details Administrator** role. Only that role may enter or change bank accounts (manual section 2.1 roles table and section 2.8). |
| 2.2 | Questionnaire → **Bank Details** → **Edit** | Enter the account. For an IBAN country only the **IBAN** is needed; the manual says the SWIFT code "does not have to be inserted as it will be automatically generated by the system when the bank account is approved by ESA". |
| 2.3 | Same section, attachment arrow | Upload the bank account confirmation (section 0). |
| 2.4 | Same section → **Submit** | Submit the account for approval by the **ESA Finance User**. Status moves from *In preparation* to *Submitted for validation*, then *Published* on approval or *Sent back* for clarification. |
| 2.5 | — | Double-check the IBAN before submitting: the manual says the IBAN "cannot be modified" after ESA validates it. A wrong one means creating a new account record and deactivating the old one. |
| 2.6 | If validation seems overdue | The manual gives ESA's Finance Department address, `vendordata@esa.int`. |

**Full registration also needs financials, which the bank account does not supply.**
The manual lists, for Full registration: **Owners of Shares / Voting Rights Details**
(must total 100%), **Contacts** (Chief Executive Officer and Chief Financial Officer for
a company), **Other Financial Indicators and Staff** with an upload of the **Latest
Approved Financial Statement**, **Compliance** (Article 12 of the ESA Contracting
Regulations) and **Corporate Social Responsibility**. Light registration already asks
for **Financial and Staff Basics**, where an entity without figures yet may declare
that and commit to provide them. Whether the company has an approved financial
statement to upload before its first financial year closes is a question for the
accountant; it is not answered here.

The esa-star entity identifier recorded in `legal/COUNSEL-REVIEW-PACKAGE.md` is 83208;
check it in esa-star rather than trusting this copy.

## 7. Other systems

- [ ] Accounting / bookkeeping software: add the account so bank statements reconcile.
- [ ] Any online payment processor adopted later: its payout destination is this account.
      None is in use today.
- [ ] A second account at a second provider is advisable, so the company is never
      single-homed on one bank. If one is opened, it gets its own `[bank]` values only
      if invoices should name it; the renderer prints exactly one account.
