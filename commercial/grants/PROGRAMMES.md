## 1. ESA NAVISP

### Structure and funding

- Three elements: Element 1 innovation, Element 2 competitiveness, Element 3 support to Member States [S1].
- Element 1 "will call for a fully funded approach", via competitive tenders; no percentage stated [S1].
- Element 2 "will call for a co-funding approach to be envisaged"; **co-funding rate UNKNOWN** (not stated on [S1] or [S3]).
- Element 3 is institutional and fully funded; partnership mechanisms should cover "100% of ESA's costs" [S1].
- Funding size: the NAVISP homepage states zero-equity funding "from €60k to €2M per activity" [S2]. The split by element is **UNKNOWN**.

### Estonia: participation and delegation (conflict)

- ESA states Estonia became a full Member State: 21st, 1 September 2015 [S5]. ESA's list of 23 current Member States includes Estonia [S5].
- The NAVISP homepage shows 27 flags including Estonia [S2]. The country page for Estonia lists "No ongoing projects" and no completed projects shown [S4].
- **Conflict:** the NAVISP National Delegations page lists 22 countries and Estonia is **not** among them [S6]. The Element 2 standard call lists eligible countries AT, BE, CA, CZ, DK, FI, FR, DE, HU, IE, IT, NL, NO, PL, PT, RO, ES, SE, CH, UK, and Estonia is **not** listed [S7]. None of the four open Element 1 ITTs lists Estonia [S8].
- Conclusion: Estonia's NAVISP subscription and delegation route are **UNKNOWN**. The founder must ask ESA, through the contact address on the NAVISP site [S2] or the Estonian national space contact point, before any effort goes into an Element 2 proposal.

### Element 1: competitive ITTs

- Route: ESA Invitations to Tender and the "Submit New Ideas" page [S1].
- Four ITTs were open on 2026-10-09 [S8]: 131 Chip Scale Optical Atomic Clock (closes 9 Oct 2026, 13:00); 130 Robust GNSS Tracker for Sensitive Applications (29 Oct 2026, 13:00); 120 Resilient PNT Critical National Infrastructure validation test bench (2 Nov 2026, 13:00); 132 Flexible eLoran/GNSS signal simulator (18 Nov 2026, 13:00). Times as printed on [S8].
- Eligible-country lists on all four omit Estonia [S8]. A letter of support is required for Element 1 proposals [S10].
- IP: Article 6 of the Draft Contract for EXPRO+ ITTs applies; the General Conditions of Contract (GCC) do not [S10]. Article text **not read**.
- Fit: none of the four is a match for a simulation engine. ITT 120 (CNI validation test bench) is closest in topic but is a hardware test bench and Estonia is not eligible per [S8].

### Element 2: open call for proposals

- Route: Outline Proposal through ESA's Open Space Innovation Platform; a favourable assessment leads to a Full Proposal [S7]. Tendering details sit in the Standard Call, which needs an esa-star login [S7] (not read).
- The call "welcomes proposals from space and non-space companies, from large and small enterprises and start-ups" [S7].
- Support letter from the relevant national delegation is required for the Full Proposal [S7]. Industry proposals must be backed by the participating State [S1].
- Open since 01/09/2020; no closing date listed [S7].
- IP: the Contractor owns the IPR in software developed in the activity; ESA receives a free licence on contract signature; software is delivered in object code only [S10]. Part II, Option B of the GCC (Clauses 50-63) applies with modifications [S10].
- **AGPL conflict check.** Ownership staying with the contractor means Ashforde OÜ can release under AGPL. The "object code only" delivery term and ESA's free licence are not in themselves a conflict, but any clause restricting publication or requiring ESA consent before open release has **not been read**. Counsel must read Clauses 50-63 and the Draft Contract before submission.
- Fit: good on content (Section 3 of this file and `OUTLINE-NAVISP-E2.md`). Gated on Estonian eligibility and a support letter.

### Element 3

- "Participating States will individually provide request of support action" [S1]; permanent call for institutional projects [S2]. A company cannot apply. Ashforde OÜ could at most suggest a national support action to a delegation. Estonia's delegation is **UNKNOWN** [S6].

### Events

## 2. EUSPA and the EU Space Programme

### Fundamental Elements (EUSPA grants)

- Funds "chipsets, receivers, and antennas" for EGNSS; budget "EUR 43 million" for 2021-2027; second round covers operational use of differentiators (OSNMA, HAS, triple-frequency), preparation for EWSS, SAS and ARAIM, and emerging technologies [S12].
- Horizon Europe covers content and application development; Fundamental Elements covers chipset and receiver technology [S12].
- Calls seen [S13][S14]: EUSPA/GRANT/01/2026 PRS receiver box (EUR 2,000,000; deadline moved from 15 Jul to 31 Jul 2026; status Closed); EUSPA/GRANT/02/2025 robust receiver (extended to 27 Feb 2026, passed); EUSPA/GRANT/01/2025 HAS space receiver (closed). The first page of the grants list showed all ten listed calls Closed; later pages not read [S13].
- **Eligibility, co-funding rate, IP terms: UNKNOWN.** The call pages point to Annexes and draft Grant Agreements which were not read [S14].
- Fit: poor. Kshana is not a receiver, chipset or antenna.

### Horizon Europe: EUSPA-managed topics

- EUSPA lists one open or upcoming topic: HORIZON-2027-EUSPA-SPACE-51, "Galileo and Copernicus contribution to the EU Preparedness Union Strategy", fifth EUSPA Horizon Europe call, Work Programme 2026-2027 page 288. Dates, budget, rate and eligibility **UNKNOWN**; to be published on the Funding & Tenders portal [S15].
- The previous call HORIZON-EUSPA-2026-SPACE closed 24 February 2026; EUR 15 million over two topics (space data economy EUR 10 million, European resiliency EUR 5 million). Open to "companies of all sizes, academia, and public organisations"; the resiliency topic needs at least two crisis or security organisations from two EU Member States [S16].
- Fit: weak. The topic family is applications using Galileo and Copernicus services.

### Horizon Europe: HaDEA Cluster 4 Space

## 3. NATO DIANA

All DIANA facts below come from the Estonian accelerator operator's page [S9] unless stated; `diana.nato.int` could not be read.

- Programme: six-month accelerator, starting January 2027 for the 2027 cohort [S9].
- Challenge areas for the 2027 call: human survivability; multidomain autonomy of uncrewed systems; multidomain sensing and advanced data processing; **operational resilience in contested environments**; responsive logistics; scalable countermeasures for air defence [S9]. Kshana's PNT resilience work maps to the fourth.
- Funding: "€100,000 in contractual funding" for selected companies; access to "over 200 test centres" and a mentor network [S9]. Additional funding beyond the accelerator (reported as up to EUR 300,000) is **not confirmed by an official page I could read**.
- Call timing: the open call closed on 3 July (the page gives no year; the 2027 cohort implies 2026) [S9]. Next call date **UNKNOWN**.
- Application route: NATO DIANA's proposal portal [S9].
- Estonian accelerator: run by Tehnopol with Sparkup Tartu Science Park, funded by the Estonian Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications with support from the Ministries of Foreign Affairs and Defence and the City of Tallinn [S9]. Applicant webinars were held (19 June) [S9].
- Estonian test centres: **UNKNOWN from an official page**. One article lists TalTech, Foundation CR14 and others, but I could not confirm it from an official source.
- Eligibility: the operator invites "Estonian and other NATO-based deep tech companies" [S9]. Ownership, headquarters and control conditions: **UNKNOWN**. A third-party guide claims ownership and control conditions; treat as unverified.
- Co-funding: **UNKNOWN**. Contractual funding implies no co-funding requirement, but this is not stated.
- IP terms: **UNKNOWN**. Must be read from the DIANA call terms before any commitment. Check specifically: ownership of results, any government-use or security-classification rights, any restriction on publishing or open release.
- Fit: good on content and nationality. Open risks: IP terms, export-control status of the software, and the defence-end-use framing of the challenge.
