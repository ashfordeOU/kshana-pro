# Request for proposals: independent technical review of a receiver trust-score method

**Draft for founder approval. Not issued.** Issuer: Ashforde OÜ, a private limited company in Tallinn, Estonia. Contact: `[FOUNDER: insert a role-based address, not an individual's name]`. Date issued: `[PLACEHOLDER]`.

## 1. Background

Kshana is an open-source (AGPL-3.0) Rust engine for simulating GNSS and PNT resilience, with a public repository at https://github.com/ashfordeOU/kshana. It includes a method that scores a GNSS receiver log against pre-stated events, using monitors on signal and navigation data, and maps each epoch to a nominal, degraded or untrusted state. The method is described in `docs/RECEIVER-TRUST.md` in that repository.

Ashforde OÜ wants an independent review of that method, to be published.

## 2. What we ask

Review the method against the questions in the accompanying scope document ([`AUDIT-SCOPE.md`](AUDIT-SCOPE.md)): pre-registration, monitor logic, score mapping, test evidence and limitations. Produce a report and a finding register as listed there.

## 3. Who may respond

Universities, research groups, national metrology or test institutes, and accredited or established independent test houses with demonstrated expertise in GNSS signal processing, receiver testing, integrity monitoring or interference and spoofing assessment.
