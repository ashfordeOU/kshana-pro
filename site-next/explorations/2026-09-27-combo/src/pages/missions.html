05Who it is for

A way in for every role.

Eight kinds of user, each with the entry point, the command they would reach for first, and what Pro or custom work adds for them.

{{ROLES}}

Kshana drives no radio hardware and transmits nothing. It includes a software GNSS receiver for recorded and generated IQ, and can write synthetic baseband IQ files, but it is not RF signal-generator hardware and not a hardware or certified GNSS receiver. It is a study and trade-off instrument: it simulates what jamming, spoofing and signal loss do to a navigation and timing solution, and tells you which of its answers are validated. Not a flight product.
