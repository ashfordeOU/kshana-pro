# D10: NWPR C/N0 reads low at high per-prompt C/N0·T (L2C 20 ms, B1C 10 ms)

Status: diagnosed 2026-10-08 by W5; decision (A): document, no estimator calibration. Docs PR on the public repo (docs/iq-d10-nwpr-limits, stacked on the D8+D9 PR).
Owner: defects lane.

## Original finding
Signal matrix v2: GPS L2C CM at 20 ms (after D9) NWPR −1.76 dB, BeiDou B1C at 10 ms NWPR −2.45 dB (M2M4 in band). First guess: scene versus replica sampling of the BOC parts.

## Cause (two separate things)
1. **NWPR reads low under the loop's own PLL jitter.** NWPR is unbiased on ideal prompts at any per-prompt SNR ρ = C/N0·T (Monte Carlo, K = 20: +0.02 dB, std 0.32 dB at 10 windows, 0.14 dB at the default 50). It reads low when the K-prompt coherent sum loses coherence, and the sensitivity grows with ρ (1° RMS phase wobble: −0.06 dB at ρ = 32, −0.40 dB at ρ = 316). The PLL's own jitter is such a wobble: about 8 dB × Bn·T (ideal-loop value 4.6 dB × Bn·T). Measured on L2C CM, 45 dB-Hz, T = 20 ms, pure second-order PLL, nominal 41.99 dB-Hz:

   | PLL Bn (Hz) | Bn·T | NWPR error (dB) |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | 0.02 | −0.24 |
   | 2.5 | 0.05 | −0.44 |
   | 5 | 0.1 | −0.77 |
   | 10 | 0.2 | −1.58 |

   DLL bandwidth does not matter (0.5 and 2 Hz: 41.27 and 41.22 dB-Hz). M2M4 is insensitive (42.71 to 42.72 dB-Hz in every row). The default D9 design (FLL-assisted 5/5 Hz) reads −1.0 ± 0.3 dB over 4.5/6/9/14 s scenes (40.67/41.17/41.15/40.87 dB-Hz); the 4.5 s matrix scene sits at the low end, which is why T5 missed. No pull-in transient (no trend with scene length). The D9 clamp moved B1C from NWPR 42.55 (15/10 Hz) to 43.31 dB-Hz (10/10 Hz).
2. **BOC(1,1) at 5 MS/s loses about 1 dB of prompt power to band-limiting** (physical, not an estimator defect). BeiDou B1C data, acquisition statistic 413/451/508/523 at 5/7.3/10/20 MS/s; M2M4 43.66/44.25/44.65/44.18 dB-Hz against 45 nominal. NWPR stays 43.3 to 43.55 at every rate: the deficit that remains at 20 MS/s is cause 1.

## Impact
Code lock uses the NWPR C/N0 at 26 dB-Hz, where the effect is negligible. Reported high-C/N0 values read about 1 dB low at the default `bn_t_max = 0.1`.

## Decision
(A): documented in `docs/design/iq-notes/receiver.md` (C/N0 estimator limits), `docs/design/LOOP-DESIGN-TOML.md` and the CHANGELOG Known limitations. The FINDING texts on `gps_l2c` (T5) and `beidou_b1c` in `tests/iq_signal_matrix.rs` now state these causes; both stay ignored, no bar changes. Rejected: (B) an empirically fitted correction to a reported value; (C) bn_t_max = 0.05 (trades dynamics margin that no test measures).
Follow-up: where the campaign scores C/N0 degradation, add an M2M4 column (W7 to confirm whether it uses NWPR).
