- `fd_jacobian` is a central difference with step 1e-6.
- The forward model (`lunar_combination::forward`, step 2) computes Earth-station → lunar-satellite ranges of about 4e8 m and subtracts their nominal values.
- 1 ULP = 2^-24 = 5.96e-8 m. Divided by 2e-6 that gives 0.0298, the Mac Δ (0.0596 is 2 ULP).
- So these Jacobians move by about 3e-8 of their 1e6 scale across hosts.

- `H_hat` and `r_hat` follow `x_hat`.
