## C/N0 profiles (approved F2 work; built, PR to open)

**Module:** `iq::channel::cn0_profile`.
- Types: `Cn0Shape {Step, Ramp, Points, Fade{s4, tau_s, seed}}`, `Cn0Segment {prns, shape}`, and `Cn0Profile::{new, parse, offset_db, amplitude}`.
- Offsets are in dB relative to the scene's C/N0, and segments add.
- The fade is Rice with K from S4 and a Gauss–Markov z on a τ/32 grid. It is deterministic in (seed, sat, t).

**Wiring and surfaces:**
- `Cn0ProfileChannel::new(profile, inner)` is a `SceneChannel` that scales path amplitudes over any channel. The scene core is unchanged.
- `ProfiledTruth` adds the offset to the truth's `cn0_dbhz`.
- CLI `iq scene --cn0-profile <toml>`; Python `iq_scene(cn0_profile="<toml>")`.

**Wording:** strictly "C/N0 profile", never interference or jamming.

**Evidence:** `tests/iq_cn0_profile.rs`.
- Fade S4 within 1.3 % of target at 0.3, 0.7 and 1; mean intensity 1.
- The truth equals the stated C/N0 plus the profile.
- The tracked NWPR C/N0 drops 6.26 dB for a 6 dB step.
- Plus a Python test and stubtest, run locally against a maturin build.

**Proposed ledger row:** *C/N0 profiles in synthetic scenes*, Modelled. Bar: fade S4 within 8 %; truth exact; tracked step within 0.7 dB.

W7 will swap its test-only channel closure for `Cn0ProfileChannel` once this lands (the signature was sent to W7). W2's labfit design depends on it.
