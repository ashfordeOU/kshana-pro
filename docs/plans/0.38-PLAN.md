# Kshana 0.38.0: build plan

Status: DRAFT for owner approval, 18 Oct 2026.
Sources: docs/strategy/2026-10-18-kshana-standard-strategy.md (research memo), the 0.36/0.37 promo gap review, and the follow-ups found while releasing 0.37.
Rules carried from 0.37: one definition of done before the tag, nothing VALIDATED without an external oracle, pre-registered bars are never loosened, no transmit path, advisory statement on every surface, commits as Ashforde OÜ only.

Theme: **"the neutral referee, made visible."** 0.38 delivers the first strategy moves (Jammertest scoring, multi-vendor receiver input, timing HIL ingest), plus the Studio views the promo showed but Studio does not yet have.

## Workstreams

The work runs as 4 worker sessions at most, plus the coordinator and Founder Q&A, for 6 of the 8 allowed sessions.

What exists today: the Training page plays back a track and timeline; the Trust page scores logs, with strips, reasons and geometry numbers; the Geometry page has a skyplot with a mask and a canyon brush.

## Definition of done (summary; the full table is copied from 0.37 when work starts)
- Every new command and kind is on every surface (CLI, Rust, Python, WASM/npm, MCP, plugins, Studio, docs/SURFACES.md), or marked N/A with a reason.
- Counts are regenerated once at the final SHA.
- Site and Studio are ported, with screenshots at 1440 and 390 px in light and dark.
- CHANGELOG, README "What's new in 0.38", CITATION and Zenodo notes are done.
- One full CI run is green; the owner tags; release.yml is green through parity and site.
