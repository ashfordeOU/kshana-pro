## 1. Interference-monitoring network capabilities

A fixed-station GNSS interference-monitoring network (SkySweeper-class: receivers at known positions, known satellite
states, environmental modelling, and a chain of ingest, model and predict, detect, characterise and locate) answers
three operator questions: can I trust my GNSS now; is it jamming, spoofing or a natural effect; where is it coming
from. Kshana already has the parts as separate modules (`receiver_trust`, maritime trust score, OSNMA, `impairment_eval`,
`space_weather`, IQ-layer scintillation/multipath/NLOS channels, `geolocation.rs` TDOA/FDOA with its accuracy bound,
`crpa.rs`, `monitor_network.rs` sizing built for the Moon, the 0.40 emitter region). What it lacks is the network-level
system for a real site, and anything checked against real ground truth. Kshana's role is the independent referee,
planner and consequence model next to such a network, not a competitor.

### 1a. What to build (order agreed)

1. **Monitoring-network planner** (first). Input a site's layout (a port, an airport), candidate station sites and a threat
   set (jammer power, likely positions). Output detection-probability and location-accuracy maps for TDOA, power
   difference, AoA and hybrids, and the recommended station count and placement. Reuses `geolocation.rs` and its
   accuracy bound, `monitor_network.rs` moved from the Moon to Earth, and the emitter-region line-of-sight model. The Pro
   design optimiser gives the Pareto front of layouts (cost, coverage, accuracy).
2. **Location metrics in the referee** (first, with 1). Score a network's detections and locations against ground truth:
   detection latency, missed and false alarms, location error, whether the stated confidence region contains the truth,
   time to locate. Extends the Jammertest scoring and `kshana partner check`. A partner's claims can become PARTNER, later
   VALIDATED, ledger rows.
3. **Fixed-station emitter locator** (second). Stations' C/N0 and AGC logs (formats Kshana already reads) in; probability
   region, ellipse and timeline out, from a power-difference fit on the emitter-region grid; TDOA where stations have IQ
   snapshots. Fixed stations are dense where crowd ADS-B/AIS reports were too sparse (the 0.40 NOT ASSESSABLE result).
   Pre-registered against Jammertest 2026 published transmitter positions.
4. **Jam / spoof / meaconing / natural benchmark** (third). Extend `impairment_eval` and kshana-bench into a multi-class
   task with natural confounders (scintillation, solar radio bursts, multipath/NLOS, obstruction), scored as a confusion
   matrix plus per-class ROC. Labelled data: TEXBAT and OAKBAT (spoofing), Jammertest logs (jamming, spoofing,
   meaconing), documented solar-burst and scintillation event days.
5. **Area trust state** (in parallel). One answer per site (trusted, degraded, under attack) from several known-position
   reference stations: per station, position error against the surveyed point, clock offset, C/N0 against an elevation
   template, OSNMA status; with alert latency. Builds on `receiver_trust` and the maritime trust score.
6. **Port scenario pack** (in parallel). Terminal timing (PTP/NTP grandmaster holdover), automated cranes and vehicles,
   pilotage and AIS under scheduled jamming or spoofing. Output: how long timing and positioning stay within budget and
   which clock buys the most margin (Kshana's best-validated area).

### 1b. How Kshana complements such a network

- **Independent referee:** item 2, published with pre-registered bars, so customers and agencies can trust a figure
  without trusting the vendor.
- **Pre-deployment planning per site:** item 1, a design and sales tool for every new site.
- **Synthetic data with known truth:** reproducible baseband IQ and receiver logs for jamming, spoofing, meaconing and
  natural effects, exact labels, same seed same file, to train and regression-test a classifier (the IQ layer already
  writes baseband files).
- **Consequence layer:** after an alert, how long the operation stays within its timing and positioning budget (item 6).
- **Environmental baseline:** expected natural degradation for a site and time from the space-weather and scintillation
  models, to cut false alarms and explain events.
- **Two-way data:** network alerts feed the on-board maritime trust score as outside evidence; ships' trust scores feed
  the network as extra observations (fixed plus crowd).

### 1c. Guardrails

- **Independence:** where Kshana scores a partner's system, the partner's data is labelled PARTNER, the scoring method is
  public, and data access is kept separate from any commercial arrangement. A commercial relationship never changes a
  verdict.
- **Wording:** the public repository stays neutral: no company names, no actors or intent; "emitter region" and
  "interference event". Company names appear only in private material.
- **Data:** partner logs under a data agreement; published outputs aggregate only; identifiers in crowd data never stored.
- **Outreach:** a concrete offer would be the per-site planner plus an independent scorecard on a public dataset. Whether
  and how to approach is the owner's call.

## 2. Lunar orientation check against NASA SVS

Compute the sub-Earth point (libration in longitude and latitude), sub-solar point, position angle of the Moon's
axis and illuminated fraction from Kshana's DE440 lunar orientation, and compare them over one year at hourly
steps with the NASA Scientific Visualization Studio Moon phase and libration tables. Pre-register the bars
(degrees, per quantity) before the comparison. A met bar adds a VALIDATED ledger row under the lunar frame chain
that the surface, landing-site, lunar-time and LunaNet kinds rely on. Small build; independent public reference.
