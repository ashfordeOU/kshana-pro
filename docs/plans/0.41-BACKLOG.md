## 1. Interference-monitoring network capabilities

A fixed-station GNSS interference-monitoring network (SkySweeper-class: receivers at known positions, real-time
detection, characterisation and location) needs an independent referee, a planner and labelled test data. Five
capabilities, in this order:

| # | Capability | What it does | Ground truth for VALIDATED |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Location scoring in the referee | Location error, confidence-region coverage and time to locate, added to the existing receiver-log scoring against Jammertest schedules | Any network with known transmitter positions |
| 2 | Monitoring-network planner | Detection-probability and location-accuracy maps per site for TDOA, power difference, AoA and hybrids; recommended station count and placement; Pro design optimiser gives the Pareto front of layouts | A deployed network's measured performance |
| 3 | Fixed-station emitter locator | Probability region, ellipse and timeline from stations' C/N0 and AGC logs; TDOA where IQ snapshots exist; never a bare point | Jammertest 2026 published transmitter positions; partner field events |
| 4 | Jam / spoof / meaconing / natural benchmark | A kshana-bench task with natural confounders (scintillation, solar radio bursts, multipath, obstruction), scored as a confusion matrix | TEXBAT, OAKBAT, Jammertest, partner-labelled events |
| 5 | Area trust state | One state per site (trusted, degraded, under attack) from several known-position reference stations, with alert latency | Partner reference-station logs |

Also: a consequence chain for a port or terminal (jamming event to timing holdover margin and positioning drift),
reusing the clock and holdover kinds; and two-way data with the on-board maritime trust score.

Wording: describe emitter regions and interference events; never intent or actors.

## 2. Lunar orientation check against NASA SVS

Compute the sub-Earth point (libration in longitude and latitude), sub-solar point, position angle of the Moon's
axis and illuminated fraction from Kshana's DE440 lunar orientation, and compare them over one year at hourly
steps with the NASA Scientific Visualization Studio Moon phase and libration tables. Pre-register the bars
(degrees, per quantity) before the comparison. A met bar adds a VALIDATED ledger row under the lunar frame chain
that the surface, landing-site, lunar-time and LunaNet kinds rely on. Small build; independent public reference.
