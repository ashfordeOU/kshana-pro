# Kshana 0.36 scope audit: every planned future item

- **Audited:** `origin/release/0.36.0-rc` @ `e0536260`, plus `kshana-pro` default branch @ `621029c6`.
- **Date:** 2026-10-12.
- **Mode:** read-only, nothing changed in either repo except this file.
- **Method:** five auditors in parallel, one per source group. Each item was checked against `src/`, `tests/`, `xval/` and the CHANGELOG. Effort is in **hours for one strong engineer, tests and docs included**, and is an estimate.
- **Appendices A–E:** they hold the full per-item records, with every requested field: id, title, file:line, status, remains, effort, data and licence, partner, oracle, dependencies and module.
- **This front section:** de-duplicates the items, groups them into work packages, and gives the plan.

## 1. Headline

1. **The docs are badly out of date.**
   - About 30 items listed as open are already in the code. Examples:
     - ITU-T masks and telecom-timing ingest;
     - the 24 h holdover scenario;
     - Lense–Thirring;
     - the precise GCRS frame chain;
     - multi-fault ARAIM;
     - the Orekit propagator cross-check;
     - single-SV RAIM FDE;
     - adaptive nulling (as a library);
     - CAI fringe-ambiguity limits;
     - 6-DoF attitude dynamics with a quaternion state;
     - the SPICE reader;
     - DE440 lunar libration (`lunar_orientation`);
     - Jd2 in SGP4;
     - the DUT1 predictor;
     - all seven GNSS-IQ streams;
     - the lunar-station pending sections B, D and E;
     - `PositioningFom`;
     - the 3-D globe in the playground.
   - One docs-only worker can correct these in about **15–20 h**. Do this first, so other workers do not rebuild what exists.
2. **About 230 distinct engineering items remain after de-duplication.** The same feature often appears in 3–5 sources. For example, NRLMSISE-00 appears as RM-10, FUT-08, DS-PA-13, CAP-01b and CAP-26.
3. **Engineering-only open work is about 2,600 h.** That excludes partner, legal and external items and the optional "stretch" items marked below.
   - With 12 parallel workers that is about 215 h each, which is **5–6 weeks of human-engineer time per worker**.
   - Add review, integration and a release-candidate soak, and the **realistic wall-clock is 7–9 weeks for 12 human engineers**.
   - With AI workers plus human review, review becomes the bottleneck. Budget **at least 2–3 weeks**: about 12 large PRs, each needing a pre-registered oracle run and maintainer review.
4. **"Every planned feature in 0.36" is not honestly achievable.**
   - About 25 items need a data partner, legal clearance, a paid standard, a login-gated product, or a publication and review cycle. Section 5 lists them.
   - Several "remain" items are also open-ended research: terrain SLAM, BOC/vector tracking, real-data PPP/RTK, and a 6.6 m → <5 m LRO fit.
   - **Recommendation:** split the scope into
     - **0.36-core:** doc fixes, plus packages that close named roadmap follow-ons with a public oracle;
     - **0.36-stretch:** large new domains, namely AOCS control, ops scheduling, re-entry, PPP/RTK and vector tracking.

### Critical path

1. **WP-00 merged first** (about 2 days). Otherwise every worker edits the same CAPABILITY.md, ROADMAP.md and VERIFICATION-MATRIX lines.
2. **Generated-doc conflicts.** `src/verification.rs`, VERIFICATION-MATRIX.md, MODELLED-RATIONALE.md and CHANGELOG [Unreleased] are hot files.
   - Workers should add ledger rows behind per-worker sections, or send them to W1 as patches.
   - Run `gen_validation_artifacts` once per merge window.
3. **W12, W2/W3 then W7/W10.** The `Scenario`-trait migration (FUT-27) and RM-04 figure tiers touch every kind, so do them in a late merge window.
   - The longest single chains are:
     - W7: SP3 clock → PPP → gLAB parity → (★ RTK);
     - W11: LABFIT B7.1 → 50-campaign bar-b run;
     - W2: MSIS → drag-tier cross-check.
4. **Pre-registration discipline.** Every promotion to VALIDATED needs a pre-registration committed before the oracle run. That adds a serial commit → run → record loop, of at least one day per row.
