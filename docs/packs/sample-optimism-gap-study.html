Executive Summary

SAMPLE report — illustrative, generated from the open Kshana optimism-gap engine on a seeded sample grid (smaller than the published arXiv:2606.22054 baseline), with NO customer data. It reports the predicted field-vs-bench degradation (the optimism gap) for the demonstrative fused baseline: the in-distribution (bench) AUC of 0.999 over-states the out-of-distribution (field) AUC of 0.908 by an optimism gap of 0.091 of the AUC range. Numbers are MODELLED over a synthetic, parameter-grounded corpus (never field/IQ) and are an engineering study, not a certification. The phenomenon and the in-distribution-only gap predictor are established in arXiv:2606.22054, an arXiv preprint (not peer-reviewed).
