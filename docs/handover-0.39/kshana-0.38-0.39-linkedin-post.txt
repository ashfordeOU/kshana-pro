🛰️ Your GNSS receiver "passed" the jamming test. Who says, and can you check it?

Kshana 0.38 and 0.39 turn a published interference test plan into a neutral, signed scorecard for any receiver. Every score in the video is a real run of the Kshana 0.39.0 engine, on a synthetic receiver log.

📋 The plan is the published Jammertest 2025 test plan (Norwegian Public Roads Administration, MIT licence). Kshana reads it as scheduled truth for Test Area 2 and scores the log test by test:

→ 1.1.13: detected, 10 s after the planned start
→ 1.1.4 and 1.1.8: late, by 30 s and 150 s
→ 1.1.12: missed, no alarm at all
→ 2 false-alarm runs in clean time, and recovery within 1 s of each slot ending

🔏 The whole scorecard ships as a signed evidence pack. "kshana evidence verify" says ok: true. Change one byte of the summary and it fails, with the file named.

What else is new since our last post:
✅ Receiver self-report beside Kshana's own monitors. On one synthetic log the receiver raised its first flag at 120 s and Kshana its first alarm at 80 s; they agreed on 11 epochs and disagreed on 17. Neither is ground truth, and Kshana says so.
✅ A holdover test for timing hardware against ITU-T masks: baseline masks pass, holdover fails the 100 ns envelope (299 ns worst case), and the clock recovers.
✅ Standards packs that map results to the DHS Resilient PNT framework, IMO and ICAO guidance, including the requirements that are not met and those that need your input.
✅ kshana-bench, a public benchmark for interference detectors, with its misses printed in red. Bar A2 (AUC 0.766 against 0.80), D1 and R1 were not met, and none was loosened.
✅ GNSS for spacecraft above the GNSS constellations, and the Moon's orbit and surface as navigation infrastructure. All modelled, with the bars we missed stated beside the ones we met.
✅ A sixth paper: "Cislunar navigation is an observability problem" (arXiv:2610.23871).

The evidence ledger now holds 388 rows: 176 checked against independent external references, 208 labelled openly as modelled, 4 awaiting partner data. No black boxes.
