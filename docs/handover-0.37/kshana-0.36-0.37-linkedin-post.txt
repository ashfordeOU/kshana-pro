Kshana 0.36 and 0.37 are out: two releases, one goal. Every claim in the engine says how it was checked.

🌙 Lunar reference station. A reproducible study of what a south-pole reference station could deliver: coverage, accuracy, protection levels and the correction-link budget, with terrain masks, a station time lab and geodesy. Its prediction is frozen and pre-registered, with SHA-256 hashes and a scoring protocol fixed in advance. A miss will be published as prominently as a hit.

🏙️ Geometry-aware trust. Street canyons, harbours, fjords and terminals now come with skyline masks, so a poor sky no longer looks like an attack. Look angles, DOP and the error ellipse are validated against Orekit, gnss_lib_py and numpy.

📡 Real data. RTK with integer ambiguity fixing on real RINEX observations, validated at a median error of 4.5 mm, plus float PPP receiver clocks validated against IGS finals and GNSS/INS fusion validated on a public dataset.

🛰️ Space segment and interchange. Entry heating, Earth-observation radiometry, attitude control, adaptive coding and contact scheduling, plus CCSDS orbit and attitude messages and SPICE kernels, validated against Orekit and CSPICE.

☁️ New in 0.37: optical PNT through the atmosphere. Cloud and clear-air extinction, turbulence and fade probability, site diversity and outage holdover for optical ground links. It is labelled Modelled, because it has not yet been compared with a measured link.

The engine gives the same answer to the byte natively and in the browser.

175 of 364 capabilities are validated against independent external references. The rest are labelled Modelled, and every one is traceable in a public ledger.

Open source (AGPL-3.0). It runs in your browser at https://kshana.dev, with no install needed. It installs with cargo, pip, npm, brew or scoop, as an MCP server for AI assistants, or as a JetBrains plugin. Source: https://github.com/ashfordeOU/kshana

Everything in the video is the real engine running on kshana.dev. Nothing is mocked up.

Advisory software for studies and training. It is not type-approved navigation equipment or a certified receiver, and nothing in it transmits.
