🛰️ A poor sky is not an attack.

Two vessels. Both lose satellites. One is sailing into a fjord, the other is being spoofed. Their receivers report a valid fix the whole time. The scores and timings in the video are real output from Kshana's engine, run live in the browser on kshana.dev, on two synthetic training logs.

▪️ Vessel A sails into a fjord. From T+300 s the cliffs hide the east and west sky. At T+885 s the score reaches its lowest point, 87.5 (degraded), with 6 satellites and HDOP 1.24. Kshana classes the satellite losses as geometry and environment, not as an attack. In 1,201 epochs the vessel is never untrusted.
▪️ Vessel B is on a coastal passage. From T+420 s a counterfeit signal starts dragging its position towards land, while GPS quality stays 1 and HDOP stays low, so nothing on the bridge alarms. At T+421 s the C/N0 checks fire and the score drops to 53.0 (untrusted). By T+459 s it is 0.0: heading and the speed log disagree with the fix. At T+1110 s the reported position is 1,408 m from the truth.
▪️ In the masked-sky demo, half the sky is blanked. HDOP rises to 4.49 and Kshana labels it "not an inconsistency" (71.3, degraded). Then a drag-off starts and the score goes to 0.0.

That is the difference between a bad sky and a spoofer, made visible. You can rerun it yourself: same scenario, same seed, same bits.
