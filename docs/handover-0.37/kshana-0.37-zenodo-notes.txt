Version 0.37.0 adds the optical-atmosphere scenario kind: cloud and clear-air optical depth, turbulence (Hufnagel-Valley Cn2, Rytov variance, gamma-gamma fade probability with aperture averaging), site diversity and outage holdover for optical ground links. It is MODELLED: checked against an independent re-implementation of the same published formulas under pre-registered bars, not against a measured link, and it makes no external-oracle claim. Climates in the bundled scenarios are ILLUSTRATIVE inputs. No ITU-R validation values are included.

Pre-registered prediction: lunar reference-station coverage (frozen 2026-10-12). This release contains a pre-registered, MODELLED prediction of the positioning accuracy, availability, coverage and protection levels a user would obtain from a lunar differential reference station at a known south-polar site, under stated public assumptions: docs/predictions/lunar-reference-station.md, with results docs/predictions/lunar-reference-station.{region,points}.result.json. Frozen at commit c05dac1d167988c33daf7ffc0ff8567b1c193ad4, seed 42.
