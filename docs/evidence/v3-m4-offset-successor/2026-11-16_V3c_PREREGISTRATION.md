## 1. Diagnosis of the misses (from the published results only)

| Row | V3-B4 recall at 5 / 10 / 20 / 50 km (bars 0.85 / 0.98 / 0.98 / 0.98) | V3-G1 FA (bar <= 0.01) |
|---|---|---|
| V3 (seeds 5000s) | 0.861 / 0.879 / 0.871 / 0.871 | 0.132 |
| V3b (seeds 6000s) | 0.829 / 0.857 / 0.829 / 0.896 | 0.072 |
| Stage 1 alone (6000s) | 0.493 / 0.632 / 0.675 / 0.714 | 0.0006 |

1. **False alarms come from chance alignments, not from the vessel's own fixes (after V3b).** V3b
   excluded fixes where AIS says the vessel is and halved the false alarms (0.132 to 0.072), but
   1 151 of 16 000 NORMAL vessels are still flagged. V3b's reading: unassociated fixes of other
   vessels (dark vessels, offset vessels) line up at a roughly constant displacement from an
   unrelated AIS track for 10 minutes or more. The V3/V3b test checks only that the displacement
   stays inside a 3.7 km gate; over 600 s a vessel with a relative speed of 5 m/s moves 3 km, so a
   crossing track passes. Nothing tests that the displacement is **constant in time**.
2. **Recall is flat in distance, so it is not a sensitivity limit.** With 1 km fix noise, a 50 km
   offset is as easy to see as a 10 km one, yet recall at 50 km (0.896) is barely above 10 km
   (0.857). Of 1 400 offset vessels in V3b, 976 were decided OFFSET, 368 NORMAL (about 260 of them
   the 2 km cases, which have no bar) and **56 DARK**: 4 % of all offset vessels end as DARK, which
   alone keeps recall below 0.98 at every distance unless the method can reach them. The rest of
   the missing recall at 5 to 50 km (about 165 vessels before the DARK ones are subtracted) is a
   distance-independent loss. Candidate causes (unverified): too few RF epochs after onset at the
   M2 layer's accesses; displaced fixes associated with another vessel's AIS track in dense
   traffic (and so excluded at step 1); the contradiction step firing on another vessel's fix.
   The published results do not separate them.

## 2. The method (fixed now; `kshana-pro/m4-offset/v5`)

Stage 1: the open engine's committed v3 decision, unchanged (`decide(scene, &M4Params::v3())`).
Stage 2, for every identity `I` whose stage-1 class is NORMAL **or DARK** and which has an AIS
track: V3b's steps 1 to 3, its excluded own-position fixes, its seeding and its final support
gate `|v - ô|^2 / σ^2 <= G`, with these changes:

1. **Relative-motion test (new).** Over the final support (fix times `t_j`, displacements
   `v_j`), fit `v(t) = o + w (t - t̄)` by least squares. The hypothesis is kept only if
   `|ŵ|^2 Stt / σ^2 <= G` (`Stt = sum (t_j - t̄)^2`, `G = 13.816`, χ²₂ at 0.999): the displacement
   does not drift. Reason: a displaced copy of the vessel moves **with** its AIS track (relative
   velocity zero); another vessel's fixes have a relative velocity of metres per second on any
   other course or speed, which this test resolves at about `σ sqrt(12) / (span sqrt(n))`, about
   1 m/s for four fixes over 30 minutes. Parallel traffic at the same speed is not rejected; that
   is the residual false-alarm source and is stated as such.
2. **Persistence.** At least `m` support epochs whose first and last are at least `T_span` apart.
3. **Exclusivity.** A fix may support the accepted hypothesis of only one identity. When two
   identities claim the same fix, the one with the smaller relative-motion statistic keeps it; the
   other is re-tested without it.
4. **No contradiction after onset:** V3b's change 3, unchanged.
5. **Decision.** An identity that passes is decided POSITION-OFFSET. A stage-1 DARK identity may
   be changed only if its stage-1 DARK class rests solely on fixes that are in the accepted
   support; fix-level `dark_fix` flags of fixes outside the support are not changed. Outputs
   describe data consistency, never intent.

**Tuning on development seeds 70 to 79 only:** `m ∈ {3, 4}`, `T_span ∈ {1200 s, 1800 s}`, by
V3's rule (highest offset recall at d >= 5 km subject to FA <= 0.005; ties to the smaller `m`,
then the smaller `T_span`; none eligible: the lowest FA, and the miss is expected). The
development run also publishes, per distance, the **recall ceiling**: the share of offset vessels
with at least `m` post-onset RF fixes spanning `T_span` (from the generator's truth). The chosen
pair, the development table and the ceiling are committed (`V3c_dev.json`) before seed 7000 is
generated. If the ceiling is below 0.98 at 10, 20 or 50 km, the bar is not reachable on this
generator with any method of this kind; the scored run is still done once and published unless
the owner withdraws V3c first, and a withdrawal is published with the development numbers.

## 4. Bars (unchanged)

V3 section 3: **V3-B4** offset recall >= 0.85 at 5 km and >= 0.98 at each of 10, 20 and 50 km
(2 km reported, no bar); guards V3-G1 FA <= 0.01; V3-G2 DARK recall >= 0.95 and precision >= 0.80;
V3-G3 GAP recall >= 0.85; V3-G4 IDENTITY-DUPLICATE recall >= 0.95; V3-G5 determinism; V3-G6
stage 1 byte for byte the open engine's. V3c is MET only if all hold. Metrics are the open
engine's (`decided_classes`, `metrics`).

## 5. Scoring

```
KSHANA_PRO_V3C_SCORE=1 cargo test --release --test integrity_v3 v3c_scored -- --nocapture
```

run once (in the existing `tests/integrity_v3.rs`); numbers in `V3c_results.json`; a test pins
the recorded verdict.

## 6. Miss and publication

Any bar or guard not held is a miss. `V3c_RESULTS.md` gives every bar, the confusion matrix and
the recall ceiling, met or missed. The E5 product keeps classes from the open M4 v3 decision, and
stage-2 hits stay "offset candidates for review", unless V3c is met.

## 7. Feasibility (for the owner; not part of the method)

* Effort about 3 to 5 days (relative-motion test, exclusivity, DARK reconsideration, ceiling
  diagnostic). No download; runs within the existing test.
* **Risk high for the 0.98 bars.** The flat recall and the 4 % of offset vessels decided DARK
  suggest a ceiling set by how often the generator's RF layer sees a vessel after onset; if that
  ceiling is under 0.98, no offset test can meet V3-B4 on this generator. The development
  ceiling answers that before any scored seed is generated. The false-alarm guard looks
  reachable (the relative-motion test removes crossing tracks), but parallel lanes in the M2 area
  are an unknown.
