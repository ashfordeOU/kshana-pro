exists and before any of the seeds named here is generated by anyone. A bar may be tightened
after the run, never loosened. The result goes in `RESULTS.md` next to this file, met or missed.

## 0. What is already known (disclosure)

The open M4 v3 result (`RESULTS-v3.md`, seeds 3000 to 3009) was read before this file: B4 NOT MET
(offset recall 0.489 at 5 km; 0.646, 0.675 and 0.661 at 10, 20 and 50 km); of 707 missed offset
vessels, 501 were decided NORMAL and 206 DARK. Its closing note names the idea this row tests: an
offset test that uses the track formed by the vessel's own displaced fixes rather than per-epoch
misses at expected accesses. No seed named below has been generated or decided. No code of the
method below has been written or run.

## 2. The method (fixed now; the code must implement exactly this)

Stage 1 is the open engine's committed v3 decision, unchanged: `kshana::m4_logic::decide(scene,
&M4Params::v3())`. Stage 2, new (Kshana Pro, `kshana-pro/m4-offset/v4`), may change only the class
of an identity whose stage-1 class is NORMAL, and only to POSITION-OFFSET. Fix flags (`dark_fix`)
are not changed, so DARK decisions of true dark vessels are unchanged by construction.

Constants: `σ = 1000 m` (the generator's fix noise, as in M4), `G = 13.816` (χ²₂ at 0.999),
`R_max = 60 km`, interpolation as M4 (AIS reports at most 60 s apart), epoch tolerance 60 s.

For each identity `I` whose stage-1 class is NORMAL (its AIS reports form one track):

1. **Association (for exclusion only).** Every RF fix `j` is given its best AIS track over all
   identities: the track with the smallest `d²_j = |fix − prediction|² / σ²` among predictions
   that exist at the fix time, if `d²_j ≤ G`. A fix whose best track belongs to another identity is
   **not** a candidate for `I`.
2. **Candidates.** The other fixes `j` at times where `I`'s track has a prediction, with
   displacement `v_j = fix − prediction` (local east, north metres) and `|v_j| ≤ R_max`.
3. **Epochs.** Candidates are grouped into epochs: a new epoch starts when a fix's time is more
   than 60 s after the previous epoch's first fix.
4. **Offset hypotheses.** Every candidate `c` with `|v_c| ≥ √G σ` (3.717 km) seeds a hypothesis
   `o = v_c` starting at its epoch `e_c`. Its **support** is, for each epoch `e ≥ e_c`, the candidate
   of that epoch with the smallest `|v − o|`, if `|v − o|² / (2σ²) ≤ G_pair`. The refined offset
   `ô` is the mean displacement of the support; the support is recomputed once with `ô` in place
   of `o` (same rule, epochs `≥ e_c`).
5. **Parallel motion.** The support must contain at least `m` epochs, and its first and last
   epochs must be at least **600 s** apart (the displaced fixes move with the AIS track, so a
   constant offset is only evidence when it persists).
6. **Size.** `|ô| ≥ √G σ`.
7. **No contradiction after onset (collision-tolerant step test).** No candidate in an epoch
   `e ≥ e_c` has `|v|² / σ² ≤ G` (a fix where the AIS track says the vessel is) together with
   `|v|² < |v − ô|²`, unless that same epoch also has a support candidate. (A fix where AIS says
   the vessel is, with no fix at the offset in that epoch, means the AIS position was confirmed
   after the supposed onset.)
8. **Decision.** If any hypothesis passes 5 to 7, `I` is decided POSITION-OFFSET. Every output
   describes data consistency ("AIS position inconsistent with a persistent, parallel RF track"),
   never intent.

**Tuning on the development seeds only:** `m ∈ {2, 3}` and `G_pair ∈ {13.816, 18.421}` (χ²₂ at
0.999 and 0.9999). The pair chosen is the one with the highest POSITION-OFFSET recall at `d ≥ 5` km
subject to `FA ≤ 0.005` (the rule of M4 v1 to v3); ties to the smaller `m`, then the smaller
`G_pair`. If no pair is eligible, the pair with the lowest FA is taken and the miss is expected.
The chosen pair is committed as constants before seed 5000 is generated.

## 3. Bars (M4 v1's, unchanged; scored on seeds 5000 to 5009)

| Id | Bar |
|---|---|
| **V3-B4 (the row)** | POSITION-OFFSET recall **≥ 0.85 at d = 5 km** and **≥ 0.98 at each of d = 10, 20 and 50 km**. Recall at 2 km reported, no bar |
| V3-G1 (guard, M4-B1) | False-alarm rate `FA ≤ 0.01` |

Metrics are the open engine's own (`kshana::m4_logic::decided_classes` and `metrics`: confusion
matrix, recall and precision per class with exact 95 % intervals, recall per offset).

**V3 is MET only if V3-B4 and every guard hold.** If V3 is met, the E5 integrity scoring may say
"the RF cross-check met the pre-registered M4 bars on synthetic scenes (MODELLED)"; otherwise the
E5 product says the offset bar is not met, with the numbers.

## 4. What MET would and would not mean

MET would mean the method separates the five synthetic classes at the stated rates on scenes whose
truth is exact, for this generator. It would not mean real vessels or real RF fixes behave like the
generator, that any flagged vessel did anything wrong (an offset has ordinary causes: a faulty or
interfered receiver, a wrong antenna offset, a configuration error), or that the method is
VALIDATED.
