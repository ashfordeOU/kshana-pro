# V2 pre-registration: incident forensics detection bars (roadmap 0.42 E1)

Written and committed on **2026-11-15 (12:25 UTC)**, alone, before the labelled set is generated
and before any scoring code is written or run. It fixes the labelled set, the method under test,
what counts as a detection, the metrics and the pass thresholds. A bar may be tightened after
the run, never loosened. The set is scored **once**; the result is published in `RESULTS.md`
next to this file whether the bars are met or missed, and a miss is not tuned away.

**E1 claim rule.** Incident forensics (E1) may be called "met its pre-registered detection bars"
in public only if every bar below passes on the synthetic part **and** the public-event part has
been scored and passes. Until then every E1 report keeps its "V2 not met" limit and no E1
detection claim is made anywhere.

## 0. What the data are, plainly

* **Public-event part (P): empty at registration.** Neither repository holds an openly licensed
  recording (receiver log, AIS or ADS-B) of a documented public interference event: the open
  repository's track and maritime samples are synthetic, JammerTest data are reached only through
  the open engine's download path and are never committed, and no commercial feed is used. P is
  therefore **not scored** in this run, and the E1 claim rule above cannot be satisfied by it.
  P is to be pre-registered separately (bars equal to or tighter than those below) once openly
  licensed public-event data with a published schedule of what was transmitted is in hand.
* **Synthetic part (S and T): SYNTHETIC, not measurements.** Receiver logs are generated by the
  open engine's NMEA synthesiser (`kshana::nmea_synth`, crate `kshana` 0.41.0) with injected
  events; AIS and ADS-B tables are generated by the scoring code from the distributions below.
  A pass supports only the sentence "on a pre-registered synthetic labelled set, the forensics
  method met its detection bars"; it says nothing about any real receiver, vessel, aircraft or
  event.
* **Independence from development.** The method was exercised during development on the five
  training scenarios (`tests/fixtures/forensics/training/`, synthesiser seeds 11 and 12) and the
  open synthetic interference-map sample. None of them is in the labelled set; the labelled set's
  synthesiser seeds start at 1000.

## 1. Method under test

`kshana-pro/incident-forensics/v1`, exactly as committed on the branch at registration
(`src/forensics/detect.rs`, constants `MethodParams::V1`). No parameter is changed for this run.
Each case is analysed with `forensics::analyse(&scenario, None)`.

## 2. The labelled set

Random draws come from SplitMix64 seeded with **20261115**, drawn case by case in the order
below; `U(a, b)` is uniform on `[a, b)`, `UI(a, b)` a uniform integer in `[a, b]`. Every case's
parameters and label are written into the results file, so the set can be inspected and
regenerated.

### 2.1 S: receiver cases (48)

Case `i` = 0 … 47 has class `jamming` for `i mod 4 = 0`, `spoofing` for 1, `clean` for 2 and
`confounder` for 3 (12 of each). Common to every case: synthesiser seed `1000 + i`, duration
1200 s at 1 Hz, `start_utc` = 2026-06-01T00:00:00Z + `i` x 3 h, vessel start latitude
`U(50, 62)` and longitude `U(-5, 25)` degrees, speed `U(6, 20)` kn, one waypoint at latitude
+`U(0.1, 0.3)` and longitude +`U(0.2, 0.8)` degrees from the start, current 0.5 kn set 180
degrees, magnetic variation 2 degrees. The incident is receiver-only: one NMEA log, platform
`vessel`, window from 200 s after the stream start to its end (as in the development tests).

| Class | Injected event (one per case) |
|---|---|
| `jamming` | `kind = "jamming"`, `start_s` `UI(250, 500)`, `duration_s` `UI(240, 600)`, `ramp_s` `UI(30, 120)`, `recovery_s` 60, `cn0_drop_db` `U(20, 40)`, `spread_db` 6 |
| `spoofing` | even `i / 4`: `kind = "drag-off"`, `start_s` `UI(250, 500)`, `duration_s` `UI(500, 800)`, `ramp_s` `UI(120, 400)`, `recovery_s` 0, `final_offset_m` `U(500, 2500)`, `bearing_deg` `U(0, 360)`, `counterfeit_cn0_dbhz` `U(44, 48)`; odd `i / 4`: `kind = "time-spoof"`, `start_s` `UI(250, 500)`, `duration_s` `UI(400, 700)`, `ramp_s` `UI(60, 180)`, `recovery_s` 0, `offset_s` `U(5, 60)` |
| `clean` | none |
| `confounder` | `kind = "natural-degradation"`, `start_s` `UI(250, 500)`, `duration_s` `UI(300, 660)`, `ramp_s` 120, `recovery_s` 120, skyline preset `fjord` for even `i / 4`, `container-terminal` for odd |

Labels: `jamming` cases are positive for jamming; `spoofing` cases positive for spoofing;
`clean` and `confounder` cases negative for both.

### 2.2 T: track cases (40)

AIS (20 cases, `j` = 0 … 19): six vessels, each reporting every 60 s for 60 reports (one hour),
start 2026-07-01T00:00:00Z + `j` x 2 h, start positions latitude `U(54, 58)`, longitude
`U(3, 12)`, straight courses at `U(8, 16)` kn on a heading `U(0, 360)`, each report with
independent 10 m (one sigma) position noise and the reported speed. `j < 10` positive (spoofing):
for even `j` one vessel's reports from report `UI(20, 40)` on are displaced by `U(2, 20)` km
(a jump that stays); for odd `j` one vessel's track is replaced by a circle of radius
`U(0.5, 2)` km traversed once in 3000 s (reported speed the circle's). `j >= 10` negative.

ADS-B (20 cases, `k` = 0 … 19): six aircraft, each reporting every 30 s for 40 reports, start
2026-08-01T00:00:00Z + `k` x 2 h, latitude `U(50, 60)`, longitude `U(0, 20)`, eastbound at
0.045 degrees per report, 35 000 ft, NIC 8 and NACp 9. `k < 10` positive (jamming): `UI(1, 4)`
aircraft report NIC 0 and NACp 0 from report `UI(10, 20)` for `UI(6, 15)` reports, then 8 and 9
again. `k >= 10` negative.

Each track case is one incident with only that AIS or ADS-B file, the window covering every
report, no area box.

## 3. What counts

A case **indicates** a category when `verdict.<category>.status == "indicated"`. The onset of a
detected receiver case is the latest onset bound (`onset.latest_utc`) of the earliest finding of
the injected category (a `jamming-and-spoofing` finding counts for either), relative to the
stream start.

## 4. Bars (all must pass)

| Bar | On | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | S, 24 positive cases | the injected category is indicated in at least **22 of 24** (≥ 90 %) |
| B1j / B1s | S, 12 jamming / 12 spoofing cases | each at least **10 of 12** |
| B2 | S, 24 negative cases (clean and confounder) | jamming or spoofing indicated in at most **1 of 24** (≤ 5 %) |
| B3 | S, 24 positive cases | the category that was **not** injected is indicated in at most **2 of 24** (cross-attribution ≤ 10 %) |
| B4 | S, detected positive cases | onset within `[start_s, start_s + ramp_s + 60 s]` in at least 90 % of them |
| B5 | S, all 48 cases | no finding at `high` confidence (one receiver is one source) |
| B6 | T, AIS | spoofing indicated in at least **9 of 10** positives and in at most **1 of 10** negatives |
| B7 | T, ADS-B | jamming indicated in at least **9 of 10** positives and in at most **1 of 10** negatives |

## 5. Procedure

1. This file is committed alone.
2. The generator and scorer (`tests/forensics_v2.rs`) are written after it; they implement
   sections 2 to 4 and nothing else.
3. The scorer is run once with `KSHANA_PRO_V2_WRITE=1`, writing `results.json` beside this
   file; `RESULTS.md` reports every bar with its numbers, met or missed.
4. The test then pins the committed outcome (re-running must reproduce it exactly).
