**Owner:** Ashforde OÜ · **Status:** working draft · **Date:** 2026-06-24

The internal sales kit for **Pack A — Optimism-Gap Audit**, a commercial study delivered
on the open [Kshana](https://github.com/AshfordeOU/kshana) PNT engine by
[Ashforde OÜ](https://ashforde.org). This kit exists to do one job, and only that job.

---

## The single goal

**Land the first paying Pack A design partner.** One reference customer who runs a real
study, gets a real number, and lets us name them (or anonymise them well) in a case study.
Everything in this kit is in service of that.

## Core positioning sentence

> Pack A is the **reproducible, honest evidence layer that PNT R&D teams trust to feed
> their own V&V** — a MODELLED study of exactly how much a GNSS interference detector
> degrades from bench to field, computed on an open engine they can audit.

We do not certify. We do not guarantee field performance. We tell teams the uncomfortable
number — reproducibly — and that honesty is the moat.

- **Honesty is the entire moat.** Every claim stays inside what we can defend.
- The deliverable is **MODELLED** (a simulation study) and is explicitly **not a certification**.
- The underlying result is an **arXiv preprint** ([arXiv:2606.22054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22054)) — **not peer-reviewed**. Never imply otherwise.
- **No** claims of TRL level, flight heritage, ESA endorsement, certification, or regulatory approval.
- We sell reproducible evidence that feeds the **customer's own V&V** — never "we certify / guarantee field performance".
- **Pricing is available on request**, per the order form. Do not quote numbers in outreach.
