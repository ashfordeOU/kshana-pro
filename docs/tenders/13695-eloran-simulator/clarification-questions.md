## §B Clarification questions for AO/1-13695 (pick 5–10; all are drafted)

Ranked by value. Q1–Q5 are the ones to send for sure.

**Q1 Eligibility of subcontractors and suppliers.**
> EXPRO/TC §1.1 eligibility applies to the Tenderer and its Subcontractors. Does it also apply to a
> supplier that provides a licence for pre-existing commercial software, without performing work under the
> contract, when that supplier is established in an ESA Member State that does not participate in this
> ITT? Can such a supplier take part as a Subcontractor at no cost to NAVISP Element 1, or are such
> supplies limited in some way?

**Q2 Pre-existing software as background IP.**
> If the proposed simulator builds on pre-existing proprietary software of the Tenderer, can that software
> be declared as background intellectual property and delivered to the Agency as executable code under a
> licence, with source code kept by the Tenderer? Or does the Agency require source-code delivery of all
> software, including background software?

**Q3 Hardware deliverable and target TRL.**
> Does the Agency expect a physical simulator unit (signal generation hardware) to be delivered to ESTEC
> at the end of the activity, or can the deliverable be the software core together with a defined
> reference hardware configuration, demonstrated in the contractor's laboratory? What TRL is expected at
> the end of the activity?

**Q4 GNSS simulators for the synchronisation demonstration.**
> Does the Agency require synchronisation to be demonstrated with specific commercial GNSS simulator
> models, or with a minimum number of different models? Will GNSS simulators of the ESTEC Navigation
> Laboratory be available to the contractor for this demonstration?

**Q5 Live signals for fidelity validation.**
> For validation against live signals, which eLoran or Loran transmissions does the Agency consider
> acceptable (for example the transmitter currently operating in the United Kingdom, or test
> transmitters)? Can the Agency help with access to transmission schedules, or to recorded data from
> earlier NAVISP activities?

**Q6 Signal definitions baseline.**
> Which signal and data-channel definitions are the mandatory baseline for the simulator (for example the
> LORAN-C signal specification, SAE AS9990-family documents, Eurofix, 9th- and 10th-pulse data channels)?
> Will the Agency provide documents that are not publicly available, or must the contractor obtain them?

**Q7 Datasets for the PNT Digital Twin.**
> The activity description mentions that record-and-replay datasets could support validation within the
> UK PNT Digital Twin (NAVISP EL3-036bis). Is there a required data format, channel set (LF and GNSS
> bands) or coherence requirement for these datasets?

**Q8 Conducted versus over-the-air output.**
> Is conducted output (cable or antenna simulator) sufficient for all tests in this activity, or does the
> Agency expect any over-the-air radiation, which would require a transmission licence?

**Q9 Threat scenarios.**
> Are LF interference, jamming, spoofing or meaconing scenarios in the scope of the simulator? If so, are
> there handling or export-control requirements for those scenarios and their outputs?

**Q10 Price and price type.**
> Please confirm the maximum price for this ITT and the price type (firm fixed price or other). Please also
> confirm whether the closing date of 4 January 2027, 13:00, as shown on esa-star, is final.
