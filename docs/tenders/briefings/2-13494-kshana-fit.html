AO/1-13494 — where Kshana stands & what we can deliver

Strong thematic fit on the ground-software half; a clear, bounded consortium-SME work-package seat; everything else honestly handed to partners.

Our honest position — read this first

What Kshana is: an open, auditable AGPL-3.0 Rust PNT performance-and-resilience simulator (dual-licensed; a commercial licence from Ashforde OÜ is available), ~TRL 3, with no flight heritage, no agency contracts and no certification. It is one component of a Navigation Digital Twin — the achievable-performance and RF-impairment-evaluation engine — that a prime can audit line-by-line.

What our role is: a single consortium-SME work-package seat — never the prime, never a mandated Key Person. We bring two adjacent ground-software work packages, an auditable open core, and a mechanical scored-criteria lift (SME + German nationality in ESA-STAR → helps the ≥3-SME quota and geo-return). We do not claim the spacecraft, the payload hardware, the on-board AI engine, or the full twin.

5Open risks & what has to be true to bid

1
Registered office (the #1 risk). Ashforde OÜ is German-nationality in ESA-STAR (eligible, top geo-return) but registered in Estonia, which is not a Participating State. Award needs a participating-state registered office → stand up a German branch/subsidiary + submit an ESA Q&A clarification by ~29 Jun. founder-gated
2
A consortium seat is the hard gate. No prime, no bid this cycle — there is no solo fallback (mandatory ≥5-operator consortium + named Key Person). Partner outreach is currently parked pending an explicit go. founder-gated
3
Open-source authorisation + BIPR. Kshana is Background IPR owned by Ashforde OÜ, dual-licensed (GNU AGPL-3.0-only open track, or a commercial licence). If brought under the open AGPL-3.0 track, OSS prior-authorisation applies and AGPL copyleft attaches; Kshana is therefore brought in under the commercial licence as proprietary Background IPR, which avoids both — no copyleft obligation attaches to ESA's or the prime's use. The third-party dependency tree remains permissive-only (no GPL/copyleft anywhere). (Commercial-licence grant terms to be confirmed by counsel.)
4
The Key Person is a partner's. The mandatory space-segment Key Person comes from a partner, never Ashforde; the founder owns the performance/evaluation WP in a supporting role.

Everything that can be built from code and public specs is built (the two WPs, the four tools, the compliance-matrix template). What remains is genuinely founder-/partner-gated: the office, the seat, the Key Person and the costing — none of which can be honestly faked.
