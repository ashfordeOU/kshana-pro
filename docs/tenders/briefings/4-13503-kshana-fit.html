AO/1-13503 — where Kshana stands & what we can deliver

We own the ground-side half of the study's core question — the defensible quantum-vs-classical PNT trade and the GNSS-denied resilience case. The quantum hardware is the partner's.

Our honest position — read this first

What Kshana is: an open, auditable AGPL-3.0 Rust PNT performance-and-resilience simulator (dual-licensed; a commercial licence from Ashforde OÜ is available), ~TRL 3, with no flight heritage, no agency contracts and no certification. For this tender it is the system-performance engine that quantifies the benefit a quantum payload buys — it quantifies, it never validates the device.

What our role is: a consortium-SME work-package seat inside a quantum-hardware-led team — never the prime, never the mandated quantum-for-navigation Key Person. Quantum sensors are modelled from published coefficients; the cold-atom interferometer accelerometer is first-principles and cross-checked to within ~2 orders of a published device. Device feasibility, maturation and TRL advancement are the hardware partner's WP.
