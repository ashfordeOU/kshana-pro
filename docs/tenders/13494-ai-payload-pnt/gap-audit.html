Capability & eligibility gap audit — Kshana (open PNT performance-and-resilience simulator, v0.20.0) and Ashforde OÜ against the AI & Digital Payload Technologies PNT Demonstrator, Phases 0/A/B1 invitation to tender. THEMATIC / TECHNICAL-DOMAIN FIT
The tender's load-bearing concept — the Navigation Digital Twin (predict PNT performance/resilience, verify algorithms) plus the explicit ML-technique characterisation & evaluation task — is almost a one-line description of Kshana. The on-board-AI and reconfigurable-payload hardware half is squarely a partner's.
FIT AS A CONSORTIUM SME
Two fundable ground-software WPs (NAV-DT performance component + ML-evaluation testbed) inside a prime-led team — strong, and helps meet the ≥3-SME quota + DE geo-return. Needs a DE seat; the mandatory Key Person is a partner's.
READINESS TO BID AS PRIME
A solo founder cannot prime a €3.5M, 5-operator/3-SME, multidisciplinary AI + digital-payload + space-system study by 9 Jul 2026. Structurally non-viable as prime.
Bottom line. This is the ground-software-richest of the two FutureNAV C3 tenders for Kshana. Its load-bearing concept — the Navigation Digital Twin (a ground model that predicts achievable PNT performance/resilience and verifies on-board algorithms) plus the explicit "Characterisation & Evaluation of Machine-Learning Techniques" task — is almost exactly what Kshana already is and does, validated and reproducibly. But the tender's centre of mass is flight: on-board AI inference, a reconfigurable digital payload (SDR/FPGA, secure in-orbit update), antennas and the spacecraft itself — hardware engineering a solo founder cannot field; and the procurement is a €3.5M study requiring a 5-party / 3-SME consortium, full tailored-ECSS coverage, a space-segment "spacecraft-as-a-service" Key Person, and space heritage. The realistic, high-value play is a consortium-SME seat owning two ground-software work packages — the NAV-DT performance/resilience component and the ML-algorithm evaluation testbed — never prime, never the Key Person, never the whole twin.

2 The two-track reality

✓ Where Kshana is a genuine asset

The ground-software half: the NAV-DT performance model + the ML-evaluation referee.

  • NAV-DT achievable-performance component — the FoM engine predicts ranging/timing accuracy, availability and integrity per scenario; the DOP/geometry foundation is validated vs gnss_lib_py 1.0.4 (Stanford). One component of the twin, not the twin.
  • ML-technique characterisation & evaluation — a detector-agnostic ROC/AUC/Pfa-Pmd referee with leakage & distribution-shift guards; metrics validated vs scikit-learn 1.9.0. Owns the SoW's ML-evaluation task.
  • GNSS-denied resilience & integrity — jamming availability, RAIM/ARAIM (validated vs DO-229E on real IGS geometry), spoof/multipath scenarios.
  • Labelled RF-impairment corpus — seeded, reproducible, TEXBAT-parameter-grounded (not raw IQ — and reported as exactly that).
  • V&V discipline — machine-checked verification matrix (a row is validated only with an external oracle); seven externally-validated islands; byte-identical reruns CI-gated.
  • Runnable & open — Rust/Python/WASM/MCP + browser playground a reviewer can run, auditable line-by-line.

✗ Why a solo prime bid fails

The tender's flight-hardware breadth & structural demands.

  • Mandatory consortium — ≥5 economic operators incl. prime, ≥3 SMEs, ≥30% to non-LSIs, ≥€500K via open competition. A solo founder = 1 of 5.
  • On-board AI + reconfigurable digital payload — edge-compute inference, secure in-orbit update/reconfiguration, SDR/FPGA. Flight/payload engineering Kshana does not do.
  • Antenna / RF payload hardware + spacecraft bus — steerable phased arrays, radiation patterns, the full tailored-ECSS bus discipline set. None are Kshana's.
  • Key Person mandate — ≥1 with extensive space-segment "spacecraft-as-a-service" experience; every WP manager a named Key Person. Founder is supporting-role, never the Key Person.
  • Heritage & PA — space-hardware heritage, Class-Gamma product assurance, flight track record. Ashforde: TRL~3, no ESA contracts, no flight heritage.
  • Timeline — building a compliant consortium as prime from scratch by 9 Jul is not realistic.
