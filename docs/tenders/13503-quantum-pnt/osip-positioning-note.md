# ESDI co-sponsored research (OSIP) — positioning note (T18)

The **complementary / fallback** play, separate from the AO/1-13503 contract and outside its
scope and evaluation. Use it to position for **future phases** and to keep an early-engagement
foot in the door if the consortium seat (T7) does not close this cycle.

## What it is (from SOW doc 005)

- ESDI **co-sponsored research** in the Platform for Research: **€90k, ≤50% ESA funded**,
  **2-year postdoc co-location**.
- Open to organisations **"bidding *or* seeking early engagement"** — explicitly a way to
  engage even if not (yet) on a winning consortium.
- **Separate OSIP application** — not part of the demonstrator tender.
- **Swiss-centric** (ETHZ / EPFL / PSI / Empa) — would need a **Swiss research link**.

## Why it fits Kshana / Ashforde

- A visibility & credibility play in the exact thematic area (quantum-enabled PNT).
- The CH angle complements the DE-seat strategy (CH is the second-most-favoured nationality).
- Pairs naturally with a quantum-clock / cold-atom research group — the same kind of partner
  Ashforde would want as the consortium's quantum-hardware anchor.

## When to pull this lever

- **Primary:** as a *parallel* positioning move regardless of the tender outcome.
- **Fallback:** if no consortium seat (T7) by ~late June, the audit's reality check says do
  **not** force a weak solo bid — pivot here and target future phases / the sister tender
  1-13494.

## Honest caveats

- It is **not a substitute** for the demonstrator contract — different money, different scope,
  different process.
- Requires a Swiss research partner Ashforde does not yet have — that link is the first
  prerequisite to action.
- Timing follows the OSIP cadence, not the tender deadline.
