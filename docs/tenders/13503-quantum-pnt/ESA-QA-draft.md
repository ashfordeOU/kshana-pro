## Q1 — Eligibility: registered office vs validated nationality (the key question)

> The eligibility conditions require each economic operator to have a **registered office in
> a Participating State** by contract award. Ashforde OÜ is registered in **Estonia**, but its
> ESA-STAR entity record (ID 83208) has been **validated with German (DE) nationality**, with
> its decision-making, R&D, and costs in Germany. **Does the validated DE nationality satisfy
> the "registered office in a Participating State" requirement, or must the legal registered
> office itself be relocated/established in a Participating State (e.g. via a German branch or
> subsidiary) before contract award?**

*(This is the gating question — the answer determines whether the German seat (T2) is
mandatory or merely de-risking.)*

## Q2 — Branch vs subsidiary

> If a Participating-State registered office is required, **is a registered branch of the

> separately incorporated entity (e.g. a German GmbH/UG)? By what date must this be in place —
> at proposal submission, or at contract award?
