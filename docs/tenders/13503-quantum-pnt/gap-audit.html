Bottom line. This is the most on-thesis ESA tender Kshana could face — a Quantum-Enabled PNT Demonstrator whose Phase 0/A work (mission analysis, feasibility, quantum-vs-classical PNT trade studies, GNSS-denied resilience) is exactly what Kshana already does, validated and reproducibly. But the procurement is a €3.5M space-system engineering study requiring a 5-party / 3-SME consortium, full ECSS-E/Q/M discipline coverage, space-hardware heritage, and a quantum-nav Key Person — none of which a solo founder can field as prime in the ~3-week window. The realistic, high-value play is to join a prime-led consortium as the quantum-PNT performance-simulation SME (and/or use the ESDI co-sponsored-research route to position for later phases). Not a solo prime bid.

2 The two-track reality

✓ Where Kshana is a genuine asset

The navigation-performance & trade-study heart of Phase 0/A.

  • Quantum-vs-classical PNT performance — the demonstrator's central question; Kshana's whole purpose (clocks, cold-atom inertial, optical time transfer, scored FoM).
  • GNSS-denied resilience — jamming, multi-layer spoofing, alt-PNT (gravity/terrain/magnetic), integrity (ARAIM/SBAS).
  • Mission analysis — constellation design, coverage/DOP/availability, SGP4/Cowell propagation, orbit determination.
  • Timing — Allan suite, holdover, TWSTFT/optical/PPP transfer, ensemble timescale.
  • V&V discipline — validated vs AIAA/SOFA/NIST/ESA SP3; reproducible; honest maturity ledger — ECSS-friendly evidence culture.
  • Runnable & open — Rust/Python/WASM/MCP + browser playground a reviewer can actually run.

✗ Why a solo prime bid fails

The procurement's structural & breadth demands.

  • Mandatory consortium — ≥5 economic operators incl. prime, ≥3 SMEs, ≥30% to non-LSIs, ≥€500K via open competition. A solo founder = 1 of 5.
  • Full ECSS-E breadth — AOCS, thermal, structures, mechanisms, propulsion, RF/payload, EEE/power. Kshana covers none of these.
  • Quantum-hardware design/maturation — Kshana models performance from published coefficients; it doesn't design or mature quantum payloads.
  • Key Person mandate — ≥1 with extensive experience developing quantum technologies for navigation; every WP manager a Key Person.
  • Heritage & PA — space-hardware heritage, Class-Gamma product assurance, flight track record. Ashforde: TRL~3, no ESA contracts, no flight heritage.
  • Timeline — building a compliant consortium as prime from scratch by 9 Jul is not realistic.
