## 1. The partner archetypes a winning consortium needs (both tenders)

A compliant FutureNAV C3 consortium needs ≥5 operators (incl. prime), ≥3 SMEs, ≥30 % budget to
non-LSI, and a mandatory Key Person. The discipline coverage maps to these archetypes:

| # | Archetype | Why the consortium needs it | Ashforde overlap? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Prime / system lead** | Owns SE, ECSS deliverables, submission, consortium management | No — Ashforde is a sub |
| 2 | **Hardware anchor** | 13494: RF/nav-payload + on-board-AI/edge-compute + reconfigurable-digital-payload/SDR. 13503: quantum-sensor (atomic clock / cold-atom) device + the mandatory quantum-nav Key Person | No — Ashforde models performance, not device HW |
| 3 | **RF payload / signal design / antenna** | Payload, antenna, signal/modulation chain, phased-array | No — Ashforde feeds requirements to it |
| 4 | **Spacecraft bus / AOCS / thermal / power** | ECSS-E breadth, platform | No — partner WP |
| 5 | **Digital-twin / ground-segment / data** | NAV-DT integration, ground infrastructure, data lakehouse, environment models | No — Ashforde is **one component** of the twin, consumes the environment |
| 6 | **Navigation performance & trade studies** | Quantum-vs-classical / NAV-DT achievable-performance, resilience, mission analysis, timing, AI-eval | **✅ Ashforde / Kshana** |
| (opt) | **Additional SMEs** | Reach the ≥3-SME quota + non-LSI budget share | Ashforde counts as one |

**The complementarity rule (the core selection lens):** Ashforde fits **only** role 6. A good partner
**fills a role Kshana does NOT cover** (HW, RF payload, bus, SE, ground/AI) and does **not** overlap
the navigation-performance WP. Overlap with role 6 is a *negative* selection signal.

## 2. Selection criteria (what Ashforde looks for in a prime/partner)

Applied when the search goes live (founder-gated). In priority order:

1. **Nationality fit — DE > CH.** Prioritise DE (then CH) partners to build the favoured
   geographical return (DE is the top-weighted bucket; see `sme-quota-geo-return-value.md`). A DE-led
   or DE-heavy team compounds Ashforde's own DE-SME contribution.
2. **ESA FutureNAV / NAVISP heritage.** Past FutureNAV / NAVISP / navigation / quantum-PNT contracts;
   demonstrable ESA programmatic track record; a credible path through GATEKEEPER and ISRR.
3. **Facilities.** Labs / cleanrooms / test ranges / SDR or quantum-sensor benches appropriate to the
   tender's hardware core.
4. **Complementarity — fills a role Kshana does NOT cover.** The partner owns HW / RF payload / bus /
   SE / ground/AI; it does **not** contest the navigation-performance WP. (Strongest single signal.)
5. **SME-sub appetite this cycle.** Willing and able to add an SME sub within the short (~3-week)
   teaming window before the 9 Jul 2026 deadline, via an LoI / teaming agreement.
6. **Key-Person ownership (tender-specific).** For 13503, the partner can supply the **mandatory
   quantum-nav Key Person** (Ashforde never supplies it). For 13494, a partner supplies the mandatory
   space-segment Key Person.

## 4. Negative signals (when NOT to team)

- The partner already owns a strong navigation-performance / trade-study capability (overlaps role 6).
- The partner cannot supply the mandatory Key Person (and no other consortium member can).
- Nationality mix would worsen the geo-return position (heavy non-DE/non-CH with no offset).
- No appetite or no bandwidth to add an SME sub in the teaming window.
- Any pressure for Ashforde to act as prime, a Key Person, or to bid hardware it does not own.
