# M011 (matrix row 52): Timing Protection Level under spoofing

Batch B9, branch `feat/val-b9`, worktree `~/Code/kshana-wt-val-b9`.

## Pre-registration (written 2026-10-01T03:40Z, before any comparison was run)

Oracle kind: **Measured**. Measured served-time error of the stationary u-blox ZED-F9P receiver
in the JammerTest 2024 dataset (Zenodo record 15911589, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.15910563,
GPL-3.0-or-later), derived from its real pseudoranges with independent IGS broadcast orbits and
clocks (`BRDC00IGS_R_20242550000_01D_MN.rnx.gz`, SHA-256 03a03a0d...), at the spoofing onsets
published in the JammerTest 2024 transmission plan. Same data, slices, onsets, windows, scope
(GPS L1 C/A + L2C, stationary receivers, Galileo-only sub-scenarios out) and monitors as M010
(see `M010.md`, pre-registered at the same time).

Tolerance (the plan's, fixed before any comparison): **measured time error at or below the TPL at
every detected epoch, over all spoof scenarios**.

### Definitions (fixed now)

- Detection epoch t_d: the first alarm of the M010 monitors in [onset, min(slot end, onset + 300 s)].
  Onsets with no alarm in that horizon have no detected epoch and are reported, not scored.
- Reference clock: least-squares line (bias + drift) through all accepted pre-onset clock samples
  (calibration plus pre-onset window), extrapolated forward.
- Measured undetected served-time error: max |b_t - reference(t)| over the epochs t with
  onset <= t < t_d (the time the protected chain would have served before the alarm; zero if the
  alarm is on the first epoch at or after the onset). The error at the alarm epoch itself is reported
  but not scored (it is detected, so not inside the TPL's definition).
- TPL: `tpl::timing_protection_level_ns` with q_wf, q_rw and r from the M010 calibration,
  q_drift = 0 (not observable in a 60 s calibration; disclosed), tau = 1 s, samples = 1, k = 5 and
  detection_latency_s = t_d - onset (measured from the published onset). The nominal TPL is scored;
  the `tpl_band` +/-1 decade values are reported only.

Verdict rule: PROMOTE only if at least one onset is detected and, at every detected onset, the
measured undetected served-time error is at or below the nominal TPL.

## Result (run 2026-10-01, after the pre-registration above; nothing was changed after the run)

Verdict: **DISAGREES, stays MODELLED.** At 3 of the 8 detected onsets the measured undetected
served-time error exceeds the nominal TPL.

Findings:

1. The TPL under-bounds the measured undetected error at 2.1.4, 2.3.5 and 2.3.11, by 1.9x to 5.3x;
   at 2.1.4 and 2.3.11 the measured error is also above the +1-decade end of `tpl_band`. The likely
   cause is the clock term, not the spoof: with q_drift = 0 and q_rw from a 60 s calibration, the
   coast term does not cover the u-blox TCXO's frequency wander over 15-39 s, which is the same effect
   that makes the M010 clock monitor false-alarm. This agrees with the module's own caveat that the
   TPL is only as tight as the clock's measured long-tau red-noise floor; it shows that a floor
   estimated from a 60 s window is not enough for a TCXO.
2. Three of the five "inside" results are trivial (the first post-onset epoch carries a latched
   pre-onset false alarm, so nothing was served undetected) and carry no evidence for the bound.
3. The existing calibration finding stands: the receiver itself served a large pull (about 1.01 ms
   in scenario 2.1.1 per the existing example) while claiming at most 51 ns; this run measures the
   protected chain, not the raw receiver.
