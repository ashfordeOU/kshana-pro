## Pre-registration (written 2026-10-01T03:25Z, before any comparison was run)

Oracle kind: **Library**. Orekit 12.2 (Apache-2.0, https://www.orekit.org, jars from Maven Central,
SHA-1 verified by `~/Code/kshana-oracles/setup.sh`) with Hipparchus 3.1, orekit-data `main` archive.
Orekit is given Kshana's constants (MU_EARTH = 3.986004418e14 m^3/s^2, R_eq = 6378137 m) and a
spherical Earth (flattening 0), because the row's stated scope is spherical-Earth two-body geometry.

Claim as checked (narrowed in writing now): the row's outputs launch azimuth (ascending and
descending), minimum reachable inclination, circular velocity, Earth-rotation eastward speed,
dogleg plane-change dv and the daily-opportunity count. Opportunity *times* are not an output of
`launch.rs` (it emits only the count), so the plan's "opportunity times 1 s" has nothing to compare
and is out of the claim; the count is compared instead.

Comparisons and tolerances (fixed now):
1. **Inclination from the computed azimuth.** For site latitudes {-60, -28.5, -5, 0, 5, 28.5, 45.6,
   51.6, 62.9} deg, site longitudes {0, 123.4} deg, and every reachable target inclination in
   {|lat|, |lat|+0.5, 30, 51.6, 63.4, 90, 97.8, 120, 150, 180-|lat|-0.5} deg: a burnout state at the
   site (radius R_eq + 400 km) with horizontal velocity v_circ along Kshana's ascending and along
   its descending azimuth, built in GCRF and turned into an Orekit `KeplerianOrbit`; `getI()` equals
   the target inclination within **1e-9 rad**.
2. **Minimum inclination.** Orekit inclination over an azimuth sweep (every 1 deg, plus 90 and 270)
   is never below `min_inclination(lat)` minus 1e-12 rad, and its minimum equals it within **1e-9 rad**.
3. **Circular velocity.** `|v|` of an Orekit circular `KeplerianOrbit` at a = R_eq + h (h in
   {200, 400, 800, 2000, 35786} km) equals `circular_velocity(h)` within **1e-12 relative**.
4. **Dogleg plane change.** `|v2 - v1|` between two Orekit circular orbits at the same node point with
   inclinations |lat| and i equals `plane_change_dv(v_circ, |lat - i|)` within **1e-10 relative**.
5. **Earth-rotation speed.** GCRF velocity magnitude of an Earth-fixed point (spherical body on Orekit
   ITRF, IERS 2010 conventions, simple EOP) at latitude lat equals `site_rotation_speed(lat)` within
   **1e-6 relative** (covers length-of-day and precession-rate effects Orekit includes).
6. **Daily opportunities.** Orekit event detection (a `FunctionalDetector`-style g = site_GCRF . h_hat /
   |site| on a propagator over one sidereal day, 86164.0905 s) counts sign changes of the rotating site
   through the orbit plane: equal to `daily_launch_opportunities` exactly (2 or 0) for non-tangent
   cases. Tangent cases (i = |lat| or 180 - |lat|): Kshana's 1 is accepted when Orekit's closest
   approach min |g| over the day (sampled at 10 s and refined) is at most 1e-6 and any detected sign
   changes come in one pair within 120 s of each other.

Oracle setup detail written before the opportunity comparison was run: the opportunity count uses a
site fixed in Orekit's TIRF and an orbit plane fixed in CIRF (the transform between them is the
Earth rotation angle alone), so polar motion and precession do not blur the tangent cases. The
lat 0 / i 0 and i 180 cases are excluded as degenerate (the site lies in the plane all day).

## Result (2026-10-01)

Verdict: **DISAGREES** (on comparison 5 only).
1. Inclination: 158 azimuth cases, worst |di| 2.6e-15 rad. Met.
2. Minimum inclination: 18 sweeps, worst 2.2e-16 rad. Met.
3. Circular velocity: 5 altitudes, worst 0 relative. Met.
4. Dogleg dv: 5 cases, worst 3.0e-16 relative. Met.
5. Earth-rotation speed: relative gaps 1.7e-9 (0 deg), 5.2e-8 (5), 3.1e-7 (28.5), 5.9e-7 (45.6),
   7.3e-7 (51.6), **1.12e-6 (62.9)**, 3.1e-7 (-28.5). Missed at 62.9 deg.
6. Opportunity counts: 53 cases (27 with 2 crossings, 26 with none, including every tangent case
   with min |g| at 1e-16 level and no detected crossings). Met.

Finding: the gap grows as tan(lat), consistent with a fixed pole offset of about 5.8e-7 rad between
the Earth's rotation axis in Orekit (polar motion from the IERS EOP) and the ITRF z axis that the
spherical `omega R_eq cos(lat)` rotates about. The pre-registered 1e-6 allowed for length-of-day and
precession rate but not for polar motion amplified by tan(lat). Because the claim was not narrowed
before the run, the row stays MODELLED. The geometric part of the row (azimuths, inclinations,
v_circ, dogleg, counts) agrees to rounding. A fresh comparison pre-registered either with the site
speed scoped out of the claim or with an Earth-fixed frame without polar motion (TIRF) would be a
new comparison; it cannot promote on this run.
