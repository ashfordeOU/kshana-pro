Protocol (fixed now):
- Inputs to Kshana: the reconstruction's entry-interface speed and flight-path angle as printed,
  atmosphere-relative if both relative and inertial are printed (Allen-Eggers models motion through
  the atmosphere), else inertial (disclosed). Scale height H = Kshana's own default 7200 m
  (`reentry::SCALE_HEIGHT_EARTH_M`), not fitted.
- Compared: `peak_deceleration / G0` vs the reconstructed peak deceleration (g), and
  `velocity_at_peak_deceleration` vs the reconstructed speed at peak g (relative speed if printed).
- Tolerance: **within 15 % relative** on both, for every entry obtained. Disagreement on any entry
  keeps the row MODELLED.
- Values are read only from numbers printed in text or tables. A value read off a plot is used only
  with a stated reading uncertainty, and only if that uncertainty is at most 2 % of the value.

Test (if run): `tests/reentry_reconstruction_oracle.rs::peak_deceleration_matches_reconstructed_entries`.

## Result (2026-10-01)

Verdict: **DISAGREES**. Stardust (only entry obtained): Kshana `peak_deceleration(12900 m/s, 8.2 deg,
7200 m) / G0` = 61.83 g against the best-estimated-trajectory maximum 32.89 g printed in sec. V of
NTRS 20080008567: +88 %, outside 15 %. The speed at peak g is printed only in Fig. 8 (a plot) and was
not used. Only the inertial entry state is printed, so it was the input (as pre-registered); an
atmosphere-relative speed about 0.35 km/s lower would give about 58.5 g, still +78 %.

Caveat on the oracle kind: Stardust carried no accelerometer. The 32.89 g comes from the project's
entry simulation with a 0.83 % drag multiplier fitted to the navigation state at entry and to UTTR
radar tracking at drogue deployment (sec. V). It is a tracking-constrained reconstruction, not a
direct measurement.

Finding: the constant-flight-path-angle, no-gravity Allen-Eggers solution with H = 7200 m roughly
doubles the peak deceleration of a shallow (-8.2 deg), faster-than-escape (12.9 km/s) ballistic
entry. Row stays MODELLED. Genesis and Hayabusa reconstructions were not fetched: one disagreement
already decides the verdict.
