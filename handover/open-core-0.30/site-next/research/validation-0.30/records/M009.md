## Verdict: BLOCKED (stays MODELLED). No comparison was run.

The comparison needs the jammer-to-receiver path loss, which needs the jammer position, its antenna
gain/EIRP (the plan prints transmitter power, "Power: 50W", not EIRP) and the receiver position.
Checked on 2026-10-01:

- the transmission plan (998 lines of text) gives powers, modulations and time slots only; it
  gives no jammer coordinates, antenna gain, antenna height or distances for the Bleik site;
- the event briefing (https://gpspatron.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Briefing-Jammertest-2024.pdf,
  SHA-256 4d9f3d8753995a9e5bdab1f29a295c3de6db9a8e39a9ac0f98ecd273c543fecb) refers to "Appendix G
  of the test catalog" for jammer details; that test catalogue was not found publicly (two web
  searches 2026-10-01);
- the dataset's own `scenario.json` and `info.txt` give power and zone ("zone 1") but no geometry.

The receiver position is known (nav_pvt, Bleik, about 69.2755 N, 15.9677 E), but without the jammer
position and EIRP the only way to get a predicted drop is to fit the path loss to the measured C/N0,
which is calibrating against the oracle and cannot promote the row. So no comparison was run, and no
test was added.

What would unblock it: the JammerTest 2024 test catalogue (Appendix G, jammer details and antenna)
plus the F8.1 antenna position at Bleik, from the organisers (NPRA/NKOM). A possible alternative,
which the owner would have to agree before any run, is to narrow the claim to the slope of the
J-dominated regime (measured C/N0 falling 1 dB per dB of jammer power over the documented 2 dB
ramp steps, independent of path loss). That would be a different and much smaller claim than the
row makes, and it was not run.

Proposed row text: unchanged (MODELLED); the integrator may add to the oracle text: "JammerTest 2024
measured C/N0 cannot be compared to the link budget because the published transmission plan
documents transmit power and timing but not the jammer geometry or EIRP (checked 2026-10-01)."
