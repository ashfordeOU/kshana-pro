Verdict: **DISAGREES**. Worst |rho_CW - rho_Orekit| over T/3 (all at t = T/3 = 1892 s):
C1 radial bounded 3.70e-3 m (2.55 rho^2/r), C2 along-track 6.28e-3 m (4.32), C3 cross-track
3.98e-3 m (2.73), C4 mixed bounded 1.24e-3 m (0.85). Tolerance 1e-3 m: missed in every case.

Finding: diagnostic rerun (not a promotion attempt) of the same driver at 10 m separation gives
3.70e-5, 6.28e-5, 3.98e-5, 1.24e-5 m, exactly 1/100 of the 100 m gaps: the gap is the second-order
(rho^2/r) term the linear CW model omits, growing with (n t)^2, not a defect. The plan's 1e-3 m
tolerance sits below the linearisation error itself at 100 m over a third of an orbit. Row stays
MODELLED. A future comparison could pre-register a bound of the form k (n t)^2 rho^2/r, or a
smaller separation; it would be a new comparison.
