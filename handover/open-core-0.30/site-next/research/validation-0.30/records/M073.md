# M073 (row 135) Operational-style Earth-orientation prediction error. Verdict: DISAGREES

- Oracle (Reference): archived IERS Bulletin A predictions scored against the same finals (178 issues, as M034), and the 2nd EOP PCC (Sliwinska-Bronowicz et al., Earth Planets Space 2022, doi:10.1186/s40623-022-01753-9, CC BY 4.0) day-10 UT1-UTC MAE range 0.36-3.13 ms.
- Under test: `frame_eop::archived_vintage_comparison` with the default `OperationalPredictorConfig` (15-day window), fitted only on as-issued rapid rows (the issue's own and two earlier issues' combined-EOP tables, nothing after MJD0).
- Tolerance (fixed before the run): MAE over all issues, UT1 and pole, operational <= 1.5 x Bulletin A at each lead 1-10 d (20 conditions) and day-10 operational UT1 MAE inside [0.36, 3.13] ms. All 21 must hold.
- Measured (operational / Bulletin A MAE): UT1 2.42, 4.66, 6.93, 9.04, 10.69, 12.06, 12.62, 12.53, 11.91, 11.05 at 1..10 d (operational 0.16 ms at 1 d to 5.32 ms at 10 d; Bulletin A 0.066 to 0.481 ms); pole 1.85, 1.80, 1.66, 1.57, 1.51, 1.49, 1.50, 1.53, 1.56, 1.60. Day-10 UT1 MAE 5.32 ms, outside the PCC range. 19 of 21 conditions fail (only pole at 6 and 7 d pass).
- Finding: the bias-plus-rate-plus-periodic fit over a 15-day window is far from Bulletin A's class on UT1 (Bulletin A uses longer series, an AR residual filter and atmospheric angular-momentum forecasts; with a 15-day window the 27.6-day and 13.7-day zonal-tide terms are admitted at about half a cycle, which may drive the extrapolation). Not tuned: the window and term admission were left at the crate defaults as pre-registered.
- Test: `tests/operational_eop_predictor_bulletin_a_oracle.rs::predictor_mae_against_bulletin_a_and_the_pcc_range_finding` (pins the finding).
- Fixture: as M034.
- Proposed: stays MODELLED; the row's oracle text should carry the measured ratios. Related (M035 finding): the existing "agreement with Bulletin A prediction rows" figures compare against I-flagged measured rows, not predictions.
