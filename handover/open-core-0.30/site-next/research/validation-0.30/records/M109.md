# M109 (matrix row 182): Physical constants of every solar-system body

Batch B1, branch `feat/val-b1`, worktree `~/Code/kshana-wt-val-b1`.

## Pre-registration (written 2026-10-01T03:25Z, before any comparison was run)

Oracle kind (as the plan names it): **Reference**. Oracle: NAIF generic kernels `pck00011.tpc`
(IAU WGCCRE 2015 constants) and `gm_de440.tpc` (DE440 GM values), https://naif.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/naif/generic_kernels/pck/,
read by spiceypy 8.2.0 `bodvrd` (radii, POLE_RA, POLE_DEC, PM) and `bodvcd`/`bodvrd` (GM).

Comparison: for each body, the printed (in-source) decimal of each Kshana constant against the NAIF
value: GM (Body::mu), reference radius / BodyFacts equatorial radius (NAIF RADII[0]) and mean radius
(NAIF mean of the three RADII, where NAIF prints three), pole RA0, pole DEC0, W0 and Wdot (the
constant and linear terms of POLE_RA, POLE_DEC, PM).

Tolerance (fixed now): "equal to the printed digits" is read as: the Kshana constant must equal the
NAIF value correctly rounded to the number of significant digits Kshana's source prints (so a Kshana
value that rounds NAIF's longer value is accepted; a value differing in any digit Kshana prints fails).
J2 is scoped out of the claim (it would be checked only against the papers it was copied from, which
the promotion rule excludes as a transcription check). Scope of the verdict (narrowed now): the
fourteen bodies this row adds (Mercury, Venus, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, Phobos, Deimos,
Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Titan); Sun, Earth, Moon and Mars carry conventional constants owned by
other rows and are reported for information only.

Caveat recorded now, before the run: Kshana's source cites the IAU WGCCRE 2015 report and the JPL
tables, and pck00011 is NAIF's machine-readable form of the same WGCCRE report, so for the orientation
and radius constants this is close to the "transcription of a citation's constants" the rule excludes.
The integrator should read the result with that in mind.

Test: `tests/body_constants_naif_oracle.rs::constants_match_naif_pck00011_and_gm_de440`;
fixture `tests/fixtures/body_constants_naif_oracle/`.

### Pre-run amendment (2026-10-01T03:34Z, still before any comparison was run)

While writing the generator it became clear that pck00011 prints only the triaxial RADII, not the
IAU mean radius, so "the NAIF mean of the three RADII" is a different quantity from Kshana's
volumetric mean radius and would not be a comparison of like with like. The mean radius is therefore
scoped OUT of the claim. The compared quantities are exactly: GM (Body::mu, m^3/s^2) against
BODYnnn_GM x 1e9 for the body's own NAIF id (planet id nn99, not the barycentre); BodyFacts
radius_equatorial_m against RADII[0] x 1e3; pole_ra0, pole_dec0, prime_w0, prime_w_dot (converted
back to degrees and degrees/day) against POLE_RA[0], POLE_DEC[0], PM[0], PM[1]. Body::re is not
compared (for Mercury, Saturn and Neptune it is the J2 reference radius by design). Kshana's printed
significant digits are read as the shortest decimal of the value after rounding to 12 significant
digits (to remove unit-conversion noise), trailing zeros dropped. No tolerance changed.

## Result

**Verdict: DISAGREES. STAYS MODELLED.**

108 comparisons (84 for the fourteen added bodies). 72 of 84 agree to every digit Kshana prints; **12
disagree**:
- Phobos: GM 7.087e5 vs NAIF 7.088e5 m^3/s^2; equatorial radius 13.1 vs 13.0 km; pole RA0 317.68 vs
  317.67 deg; W0 35.06 vs 35.19 deg; Wdot 1128.844585 vs 1128.844759 deg/day.
- Deimos: pole RA0 316.65 vs 316.66; DEC0 53.52 vs 53.51; W0 79.41 vs 79.40; Wdot 285.161897 vs
  285.161889.
- GM: Uranus 5.7939506103e15 vs 5.7939512565e15; Neptune 6.83509997e15 vs 6.83510315e15; Pluto
  8.69326e11 vs 8.69614e11 m^3/s^2.
Likely causes: Kshana uses the IAU 2009 Mars-satellite elements (its doc comment says so) where
pck00011 carries the 2015 update; the three GM values come from other satellite-ephemeris solutions
than gm_de440. Informational (outside the verdict): Sun, Earth and Moon GM are the IAU/WGS/DE421
conventional values (differ from gm_de440), and the Mars pole and prime meridian differ from
pck00011's updated Mars model. The pre-run caveat stands: even full agreement would have been close to
the transcription check the rule excludes. Live test
`tests/body_constants_naif_oracle.rs::recorded_naif_disagreements_are_unchanged` pins the twelve; the
plan-named `constants_match_naif_pck00011_and_gm_de440` is committed ignored. Fixture:
`tests/fixtures/body_constants_naif_oracle/`.
