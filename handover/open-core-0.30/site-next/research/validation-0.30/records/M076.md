## Pre-registration (2026-10-01T03:38Z, before any Kshana run), narrowed claim

The only LNCSS statistics with an unambiguous definition in the paper are availability ("percent
visibility of at least four LNCSS satellites", STK "Min Per Day", at the worst grid point) and
failure tolerance (at least five). The comparison is narrowed in writing to those, at the plan's
coverage tolerance:

- Kshana: `LunarEphemeris::load` of `lncss_case_{a,b,c}_navi613.csv` (the published elements, read
  as MCI exactly as the `moonlight-service-volume` sweep reads them), `PositionsMcmf::positions_mcmf`
  and `lunar_service::visible_sat_positions` (the sweep's own visibility), sampled every 60 s over the
  paper's 15 days (t = 0 at the elements' epoch, the paper's scenario start).
- Grid: all points at or south of 80 S at about 1 deg spacing, as an STK 1-deg lat/lon grid lays them
  out: rings every 1 deg of latitude from 80 S to 89 S with round(360 cos(lat)) equally spaced
  longitudes starting at 0 E, plus the pole.
- Statistic: per grid point and per 24 h day, the percentage of samples with at least 4 (at least 5)
  satellites above the mask; the reported value is the minimum over days and grid points.
- Oracle values: 5 deg: A 100 / 97.9, B 100 / 100, C 100 / 100; 20 deg: A 77.51 / 54.11,
  B 100 / 100, C 100 / 100 (availability / failure tolerance).
- Tolerance (fixed now): every one of the 12 values within 2 percentage points.
- The PDOP half of the plan's tolerance is not evaluable (no PDOP printed) and the coverage and GDOP
  columns are not comparable (definitions above). Even an agreement on availability would therefore
  validate only the narrowed availability claim.

## Result

**Verdict: DISAGREES (on the narrowed claim); the plan's PDOP half is not evaluable. STAYS MODELLED.**

346 grid points, 21 600 epochs per case. Kshana vs navi.613:
- A: availability 5 deg 100.00 vs 100 (0); tolerance 5 deg 93.89 vs 97.9 (**-4.01 pp**); availability
  20 deg 100.00 vs 77.51 (**+22.49 pp**); tolerance 20 deg 50.56 vs 54.11 (**-3.55 pp**).
- B: 100/100/100 match; tolerance 20 deg 87.92 vs 100 (**-12.08 pp**).
- C: all four 100 vs 100.
4 of 12 outside 2 pp. Likely causes: OP-frame elements read as Moon-centred inertial; two-body Kepler
over 15 days against STK's unstated force model on Earth-perturbed frozen orbits; STK's 334-point grid
not reproducible from the text. LANS gives no numeric statistic. Test
`tests/lunar_service_volume_published_oracle.rs::coverage_and_pdop_match_the_published_statistics`
committed ignored (fails; ~40 s in debug).
