# M039 (matrix row 91): Earth GNSS at lunar distance

Batch B5. **Verdict: BLOCKED (stays MODELLED). No comparison was run.**

Planned oracle (Measured): LuGRE flight C/N0 at the Moon (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.16411687, CC BY 4.0,
`~/Code/kshana-oracles/data/lugre/LuGRE.zip`, SHA-256
f151dfba3321a56dd4f62a7fb5dc1f82abb389e10b6fcf9a88bf2a2cf8895a8d) with the measured GPS IIR/IIR-M panel
patterns (NAVCEN, `data/navcen/GPS_IIR_IIR-M_LM.zip`) substituted for the Airy pattern. Planned
tolerance: per-link predicted C/N0 within +/-3 dB of measured. Planned test:
`tests/earth_gnss_lunar_lugre_oracle.rs::predicted_cn0_matches_lugre_for_iir_satellites`.

What was checked (2026-10-01):
- The data are usable: `L0/TLM/TLM_RAW_*_S_OP38_0.txt` etc. carry per-epoch `svid`, `signalId`, `cn0`
  for the surface phase (OP38 onward, 2025-03-03 to 03-16, lander at a fixed site); `TLM_EPH_*.csv`
  carries only Toe/Toc/IOD, not orbit elements.
- The receiver docs in the archive (receiver ICD NIL-TN-QAS-024 2.0, product handbook
  ESC-LUGRE-HDBK-0075) give no antenna gain, pointing or noise figure.

Why it is blocked:
1. **The module cannot take the oracle's inputs.** `earth_gnss_lunar` evaluates a Walker
   constellation with an Airy transmit pattern at a receiver given as range, RA and Dec. It has no
   path for real GPS satellite positions, per-satellite transmit power or a measured
   (theta, phi) transmit pattern. Adding them is a feature, not a defect fix, so it is outside the
   batch's file-ownership rule (src edits only for a defect the oracle exposes). A per-link budget
   written in the test would be a new implementation, not a check of this row.
2. **Inputs missing from the oracle data:** GPS precise orbits for March 2025 (IGS or ESA final SP3,
   week 2356 to 2358); per-satellite L1 C/A transmit power (for example Steigenberger, Thoelert and
   Montenbruck, J. Geod. 2018); GPS yaw attitude to orient the azimuth of each panel pattern.
3. **A published finding makes the pre-registered tolerance unreachable without a fitted loss.**
   The LuGRE first-results paper (Parker et al., NAVIGATION 73(1), navi.756,
   https://navi.ion.org/content/73/1/navi.756) reports the high-gain antenna at 15.35 dBic peak, 12 deg
   half-power beamwidth in L1/E1, surface off-pointing median 0.05 deg, LNA noise figure 0.8 dB in L1,
   and states that the C/N0 estimated in flight is generally 7 to 12 dB lower than the team's own
   prediction (whose simulation was checked to 1 to 2 dB against MMS data). A budget built from
   published inputs would therefore be expected to miss by 7 to 12 dB, and closing the gap needs an
   implementation-loss term that would have to be fitted to LuGRE itself, which is tuning, not
   validation. (The figures above were read from the paper's web page through a text extractor;
   confirm them against the PDF before citing them in the matrix.)

Finding for the integrator (row oracle text): "LuGRE flight data (CC BY 4.0) and the measured GPS
IIR/IIR-M patterns are now public, but the LuGRE team reports in-flight C/N0 7 to 12 dB below its own
prediction (Parker et al., NAVIGATION navi.756), so a per-link +/-3 dB check cannot be made without
fitting an implementation loss to the flight data; the module also has no input for real ephemerides
or a measured pattern." To revisit: add the inputs as a feature in its own change, fetch SP3 for
week 2356 to 2358, and pre-register a comparison of C/N0 differences between satellites (which
cancel the common unexplained loss) rather than absolute C/N0.
