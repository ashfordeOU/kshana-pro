## Verdict: BLOCKED (licence decision and a missing input). Row stays MODELLED. No comparison was run.

Planned oracle (Measured): DAF-MIT MagNav open flight data (Zenodo record 12723700), Flight 1002,
tail-stinger magnetometer with GNSS truth; tolerance (the plan's) position error along the flight
lines within 1.5 x the CRLB the module predicts.

Why it is blocked, found when reading the two licence files the plan says to read first
(`~/Code/kshana-oracles/data/magnav/LICENSE.md`, SHA-256 de615b59da545f3d...;
`DATA_SHARING_AGREEMENT.md`, SHA-256 8b6e8672fab81881...):

1. **The two files conflict, and the stricter one excludes a commercial user.** `LICENSE.md` says
   the work is a US Government work under 17 U.S.C. 105, in the public domain, usable without
   restriction. `DATA_SHARING_AGREEMENT.md` (Air Force Acceptable Use Agreement, ver 20200211,
   Cooperative Agreement FA8750-19-2-1000) grants only "a limited personal, non-exclusive,
   non-transferable, non-assignable, and revocable license ... solely for the purpose of non-profit
   research, non-profit education, or for government purposes", forbids distributing the Data to
   anyone not working with the licensee (2(iii)), requires expunging copies at the end of the
   purpose (2(iv)), and allows publishing Data "only to the extent necessary to discuss the
   results" (2(viii)). Ashforde is a commercial entity, and a committed fixture slice in a public
   AGPL repository is redistribution. Which document governs is a legal reading, so, like gLAB for
   M025/M026, this needs an owner decision (a licence reading, or written permission from
   aia.contact@mit.edu) before the data is used as an oracle or a slice is committed.
2. **The survey anomaly maps are not fetched.** The plan notes they come from the MagNav.jl
   artifacts; nothing under `~/Code/kshana-oracles/data/magnav/` holds a map (checked 2026-10-01),
   and the flight file alone has no map to match against.
3. Stated for whoever resumes it: `src/mapmatch.rs` is a generic field-likelihood and offset-search
   layer; the comparison also needs the aircraft magnetic compensation the study flags as
   unmodelled (scope to the tail stinger), so the effort is L even once 1 and 2 are cleared.
