# M083 (row 147) Heterogeneous UTC(k) traceability-bias integrity overbound. Verdict: DISAGREES

- Oracle (Measured): BIPM per-laboratory [UTC-UTC(k)] files with published uA, uB, u, https://webtai.bipm.org/ftp/pub/tai/other-products/utcr/ (free use with citation), retrieved 2026-09-30: 102 laboratories, 105 024 rows with a value and uncertainties (rows printed "-" excluded). Every file's SHA-256 pinned in the fixture.
- Under test: `integrity::hetero_budget::integrity_bias_overbound`, U = published u, k = 1, ageing 0, tail 1e-2 (b = 2.576 u).
- Tolerance (fixed before the run): pooled fraction of |UTC-UTC(k)| > b at most 1e-2; Clopper-Pearson 95 % recorded.
- Measured: 53 306 / 105 024 = 0.5076 (CP 95 %: 0.5045-0.5106); 101 of 102 laboratories individually above 0.01 (eight at 1.0); with uB instead of u, 0.5131.
- Finding: the Circular T uncertainty is the uncertainty of the BIPM's determination of UTC-UTC(k), not a bound on the offset, which routinely runs to many times u (for example AGGO about 296 ns with u = 3.3 ns). The overbound as specified does not bound real UTC(k) traceability bias; it would need the offset's own distribution.
